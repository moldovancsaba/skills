## 1. Win-Loss Review Philosophy

Systematic review of both wins and losses is essential for continuous improvement. Wins tell us what we're doing right; losses tell us where we need to improve. Our process focuses on learning, not blame, and aims to convert insights into actionable improvements.

## 2. Review Timing and Triggers

### 2.1 Win Reviews
- **Timing:** Within 5 business days of deal closing as "Closed Won"
- **Trigger:** Opportunity stage moved to "Closed Won" in CRM
- **Exception:** Strategic wins (>$250K) may include customer feedback session

### 2.2 Loss Reviews
- **Timing:** Within 3 business days of deal closing as "Closed Lost"
- **Trigger:** Opportunity stage moved to "Closed Lost" in CRM with loss reason documented
- **Exception:** All losses require review regardless of deal size or stage

### 2.3 Special Review Triggers
- Any deal that slipped forecast commit to future period (>30 days)
- Any deal that required CEO-level approval for pricing or terms
- Any deal involving a new logo in a target vertical
- Any deal where competitor was specifically named in loss reason

## 3. Review Participants

### 3.1 Win Review Participants
- **Required:** Account Executive, CSO
- **Optional:** Solutions Engineer (if involved), Marketing Representative (for messaging feedback), Customer Success Manager (for handoff context)

### 3.2 Loss Review Participants
- **Required:** Account Executive, CSO
- **Optional:** Solutions Engineer (if involved), Marketing Representative (for competitive/positioning feedback), Product Representative (for feature gap insights)

### 3.3 Facilitation
- CSO facilitates all win-loss reviews
- For reviews where CSO AE is involved, another sales leader may facilitate
- Notes taker assigned (rotating role or dedicated sales ops if available)

## 4. Review Structure

### 4.1 Duration
- Standard reviews: 30 minutes
- Complex/strategic deals: 60 minutes
- Group thematic reviews: 60-90 minutes

### 4.2 Agenda Template
1. Deal overview (2 min)
   - Customer, deal size, product, timeline
2. Initial expectations vs. outcome (3 min)
   - What was expected at outset?
   - What actually happened?
3. MEDDIC review (10 min)
   - How well was each element executed?
   - Where were gaps or strengths?
4. Process evaluation (10 min)
   - What worked well in our approach?
   - What could we improve?
5. Competitive landscape (5 min)
   - Who else was considered?
   - What were their strengths/weaknesses vs. us?
6. Lessons learned & action items (5-10 min)
   - What specific changes will we make?
   - Who owns each action item?

## 5. Win Review Focus Areas

### 5.1 Success Factors
- What specifically led to winning this deal?
- Which MEDDIC elements were particularly strong?
- What aspects of our solution resonated most?
- How did we differentiate from competitors?
- What sales activities were most effective?

### 5.2 Process Excellence
- What did we do exceptionally well in our sales process?
- Were there any innovative approaches used?
- How effective was the handoff to Customer Success?
- What feedback did we receive from the customer during the process?

### 5.3 Replicability
- Can this approach be replicated in similar situations?
- What specific tactics or talk tracks should be shared?
- Are there any lessons for marketing or product teams?
- What should we do more of in future deals?

## 6. Loss Review Focus Areas

### 6.1 Root Cause Analysis
- What was the primary reason for loss?
- Were there multiple contributing factors?
- At what stage did we lose momentum or fail to progress?
- What signals did we miss early in the process?

### 6.2 MEDDIC Gaps
- Which MEDDIC elements were weak or incomplete?
- Where did we fail to gather critical information?
- Were there incorrect assumptions made?
- What information did we lack that would have changed our approach?

### 6.3 Competitive Factors
- Who won and why?
- What were their advantages over our offering?
- How did we position against them?
- What could we have done differently competitively?

### 6.4 Process Shortcomings
- Where did our sales process break down?
- Were there missed opportunities for engagement?
- How effective was our discovery process?
- Did we involve the right stakeholders at the right time?

### 6.5 Actionable Insights
- What specific changes will prevent similar losses?
- Do we need product changes, positioning updates, or process improvements?
- What competitive intelligence should be shared?
- What should we do less of or stop doing?

## 7. Documentation Requirements

### 7.1 Win Review Documentation
- Win Review Form completed in CRM or shared document
- Key success factors documented
- Replicable tactics identified
- Feedback for marketing/product teams noted
- Customer quotes or testimonials captured (with permission)

### 7.2 Loss Review Documentation
- Loss Review Form completed in CRM or shared document
- Primary and secondary loss reasons documented
- MEDDIC gap analysis completed
- Competitive intelligence captured
- Process improvement recommendations documented
- Action items with owners and due dates assigned

### 7.3 Standard Documentation Fields
- Deal ID, customer name, deal size, product/solution
- Date opened, date closed, sales cycle length
- AE name, SE name (if applicable)
- Initial forecast category and amount
- Final outcome (won/lost) and amount
- Loss reason (standard category and explanation)
- MEDDIC completion assessment (by element)
- Key events timeline
- Lessons learned (3-5 specific items)
- Action items (owner, description, due date)
- Follow-up required (yes/no, details)

## 8. Review Process Workflow

### 8.1 Immediate Post-Close
- AE updates CRM with outcome and loss reason (if applicable)
- AE schedules win-loss review meeting
- AE notifies required participants
- AE gathers relevant documents (proposal, emails, call notes)

### 8.2 Review Meeting
- Facilitator guides discussion using agenda template
- Participants share perspectives and observations
- Focus remains on learning and improvement
- Specific action items are identified and assigned

### 8.3 Post-Review Actions
- Facilitator completes review documentation within 24 hours
- Documentation stored in deal record and shared folder
- Action items tracked in team task system
- Summary insights shared with relevant teams (marketing, product, etc.)
- AE follows up on action items and reports progress

## 9. Monthly and Quarterly Synthesis

### 9.1 Monthly Win-Loss Summary
- CSO compiles all reviews from previous month
- Identifies patterns in wins and losses
- Quantifies MEDDIC gap frequency
- Tracks competitive win/loss reasons
- Shares summary with sales team and executive leadership

### 9.2 Quarterly Win-Loss Deep Dive
- CSO leads quarterly review session with sales team
- Reviews trends over quarter
- Identifies systemic issues requiring attention
- Develops quarterly improvement plan
- Updates battle cards, objection handling, or training materials
- Reviews win-loss process effectiveness and makes improvements

## 10. Metrics and Reporting

### 10.1 Win-Loss Metrics
- **Win Rate:** % of qualified opportunities that close won
- **Loss Rate by Reason:** Distribution of loss reasons
- **Median Sales Cycle:** Won vs. lost deals
- **Average Deal Size:** Won vs. lost deals
- **Competitive Win Rate:** % of deals where we competed and won
- **Early Loss Rate:** % of losses in qualification or solution design stages

### 10.2 Process Metrics
- **Review Compliance:** % of closed deals with completed review
- **Review Timeliness:** Average days from close to review
- **Action Item Completion:** % of identified actions completed on time
- **Insight Implementation:** % of insights leading to process changes

### 10.3 Reporting Cadence
- **Weekly:** CSO reviews win-loss documentation for major deals
- **Monthly:** CSO distributes win-loss summary to sales team
- **Quarterly:** CSO presents win-loss trends to executive team
- **Ad-hoc:** Special reviews for significant losses or strategic wins

## 11. Integration with Other Processes

### 11.1 Forecasting
- Loss reasons used to improve forecast accuracy
- Win characteristics used to refine commit criteria
- Review insights inform forecast adjustments

### 11.2 Pipeline Management
- Review insights used to improve stage conversion rates
- MEDDIC gap analysis informs qualification standards
- Competitive intelligence updates battle cards

### 11.3 Enablement and Training
- Win reviews inform best practices for training materials
- Loss reviews identify skill gaps for coaching
- Both win and loss reviews update objection handling library
- New hire onboarding includes win-loss review participation

### 11.4 Product and Marketing Feedback
- Feature gaps from losses shared with product team
- Messaging effectiveness from wins shared with marketing
- Competitive intelligence shared with relevant teams
- Customer language and terminology captured for messaging
