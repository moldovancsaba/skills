## 2. Current Positioning

### 2.1 Target Audience
Small to mid-size professional service firms and consultancies (1-50 employees) who are overwhelmed by complex systems.

### 2.2 Need/Pain Point
Need simple, actionable solutions that are documented, repeatable, and auditable.

### 2.3 Category
Business process simplification and actionable documentation.

### 2.4 Key Benefit
Delivers systems that are simple, actionable, and ready to scale.

### 2.5 Primary Alternative
Complex consulting engagements or DIY approaches that lack structure.

### 2.6 Key Differentiator
We practice what we sell: our company operates on the same simple, documented, accountable, repeatable, and auditable standards we deliver to clients.

## 3. Market Category

### 3.1 Current Category
Business process consulting and documentation services.

### 3.2 Category Creation Opportunity
"Actionable Documentation" — a new category that sits between complex business process management and simple task lists.

## 4. Competitive Differentiation

| Competitor Type | Their Weakness | Our Strength |
|-----------------|----------------|--------------|
| Large consultancies | Expensive, complex, slow | Simple, fast, affordable |
| DIY solutions | Inconsistent, undocumented | Systematic, documented |
| Enterprise software | Over-engineered, costly | Lightweight, practical |
| Task list apps | No structure, no accountability | Structured, auditable |

## 5. Proof Points

1. We operate using our own system (dogfooding)
2. Every deliverable follows our Definition of Done
3. All work is documented and auditable
4. Clients can repeat and scale the systems we deliver

## 6. Positioning Validation Criteria

The positioning is considered valid when:

- Sales can articulate the positioning in 30 seconds
- Marketing campaigns using this positioning achieve >3% click-through rate
- Customer feedback confirms the positioning matches their experience
- CEO approves the positioning as accurate and differentiating
