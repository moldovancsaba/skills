# Checklist: Customer-to-Product Feedback Handoff

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure customer product feedback is routed to CPO with full context
**Cadence:** Per product feedback event
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Capture product feedback verbatim with customer context
- [ ] Assess urgency and impact
- [ ] Prepare handoff package: feedback, customer details, impact assessment, frequency
- [ ] Route to CPO with clear priority
- [ ] Log handoff in customer record
- [ ] Inform customer that feedback was received and routed
- [ ] Track CPO acknowledgment
- [ ] Close loop with customer when CPO provides response or decision

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked and the Definition of Done is satisfied.

### Definition of Done

- Feedback is captured verbatim with full context
- Feedback is routed to CPO with clear priority
- Customer is informed of receipt and routing
- CPO acknowledgment is tracked
- Customer is informed of any decision or outcome

### Storage

Completed checklists stored in customer account record under 04_customer/
