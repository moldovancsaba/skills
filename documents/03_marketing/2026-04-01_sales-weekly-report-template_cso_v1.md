## 1. Report Overview

The weekly sales report provides a snapshot of sales performance, pipeline health, and forecast accuracy. It is a critical tool for sales leadership to monitor progress, identify issues, and make informed decisions.

## 2. Report Schedule

- **Due:** Every Monday by 9:00 AM local time
- **Prepared By:** Each Account Executive (individual reports)
- **Consolidated By:** CSO (team report)
- **Distributed To:** CEO, executive team, sales team
- **Distribution Channels:** Email, company communication platform (Sales channel)

## 5. Report Formatting Standards

### 5.1 File Format
- Individual reports: One-page PDF or concise email
- Consolidated team report: 2-3 page PDF
- File naming: YYYY-MM-DD_weeklysalesreport_cso_vX.pdf
- Version numbers: Increment for template changes, not weekly updates

### 5.2 Data Standards
- All currency values in USD unless otherwise specified
- Percentages shown with one decimal place (e.g., 75.5%)
- Dates in MM/DD/YYYY format
- Large numbers formatted with commas (e.g., $1,250,000)
- Blank fields indicated with "-" or "N/A"

### 5.3 Visual Standards
- Use consistent fonts and formatting
- Tables should be simple and readable
- Conditional formatting for performance indicators (green/yellow/red)
- Charts optional but encouraged for trends
- Confidential information should be marked appropriately

## 6. Submission and Distribution Process

### 6.1 Individual AE Submission
- Submit individual report to CSO by 9:00 AM Monday
- Use standardized email subject: "WEEKLY SALES REPORT - [Your Name] - [Week of Date]"
- Attach report as PDF or include in email body
- CSO acknowledges receipt by 9:30 AM Monday

### 6.2 CSO Consolidation
- CSO consolidates reports by 10:00 AM Monday
- Runs data validation checks
- Prepares consolidated team report
- Distributes to CEO and executive team by 10:30 AM Monday
- Posts to Sales channel by 11:00 AM Monday

### 6.3 Archiving
- All reports archived in: /Reports/Sales/Weekly/
- Individual reports filed under AE name folders
- Team reports filed by date
- Retention period: 24 months

## 7. Review and Updates

This template will be reviewed quarterly and updated based on:
- Feedback from CEO and executive team on usefulness
- Changes in sales process or metrics priorities
- Input from sales team on reporting burden vs. value
- Updates to compensation plan or quota structure
- Changes in reporting tools or CRM capabilities
- Regulatory or compliance reporting requirements
