## Value Chain Overview

```
Customer Signal → Problem Framing → Prioritization → Requirements → Delivery → Feedback → Iteration
```

---

## Value Chain Stages

### 1. Customer Signal
**Input:** Feedback, support tickets, client conversations, market research  
**Output:** Validated problem statements  
**Owner:** Customer Team → Product Team

### 2. Problem Framing
**Input:** Customer signal  
**Output:** Documented problem with target user and expected outcome  
**Owner:** CPO

### 3. Prioritization
**Input:** Framed problems  
**Output:** Ranked backlog using Prioritization Framework  
**Owner:** CPO

### 4. Requirements
**Input:** Prioritized backlog  
**Output:** Requirements with acceptance criteria  
**Owner:** Product Team

### 5. Delivery
**Input:** Approved requirements  
**Output:** Working product increments  
**Owner:** Technology Team

### 6. Feedback
**Input:** Release to customers  
**Output:** Customer feedback, usage data  
**Owner:** Customer Team → Product Team

### 7. Iteration
**Input:** Feedback and data  
**Output:** Improved product, updated roadmap  
**Owner:** CPO

## Key Metrics

- Time from signal to prioritization: < 1 week
- Time from requirement to delivery: Per roadmap
- Feedback response SLA: 48 hours
- Customer satisfaction score: Target 4.5+
