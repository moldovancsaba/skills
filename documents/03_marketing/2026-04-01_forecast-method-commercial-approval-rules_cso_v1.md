## 1. Sales Forecasting Methodology

check uses a weighted pipeline forecast approach with commitment-based adjustments.

### 1.1 Forecast Categories
All opportunities are classified into one of four forecast categories:

#### 1.1.1 Pipeline (Best Case)
- Opportunities in Stages 1-3 (Lead Qualification through Solution Design)
- Weighted by stage probability (10%-40%)
- Represents potential upside if all early-stage opportunities close
- Updated weekly based on stage progression

#### 1.1.2 Commitment (Commit Case)
- Opportunities in Stage 4 (Proposal & Validation) with customer commitment to proceed
- Requires verbal or written indication of intent to purchase
- Weighted at 60% probability
- Updated when customer commitment status changes

#### 1.1.3 Upside (Potential)
- Opportunities in Stage 5 (Negotiation & Commitment) with strong positive indicators
- Requires active negotiation with favorable signs
- Weighted at 80% probability
- Updated based on negotiation progress

#### 1.1.4 Closed Won (Committed)
- Opportunities in Stage 6 with executed contract
- Weighted at 100% probability
- Represents actual booked revenue

### 1.2 Forecast Calculation
The sales forecast is calculated as:

**Forecast = Σ (Opportunity Value × Stage Probability)**

Where stage probabilities are:
- Stage 1: 10%
- Stage 2: 25%
- Stage 3: 40%
- Stage 4: 60% (with customer commitment)
- Stage 5: 80% (active negotiation)
- Stage 6: 100% (closed won)

### 1.3 Forecast Submission and Update Cadence
- **Weekly:** Individual forecast updates due every Monday by 10 AM local time
- **Monthly:** Formal forecast review with ExCom on first Thursday of month
- **Quarterly:** Rolling forecast update with strategic adjustments
- **Ad-hoc:** Immediate updates required for significant changes (>20% value change or stage change)

### 1.4 Forecast Accuracy Measurement
Forecast accuracy is measured as:

**Accuracy % = (Actual Booked Revenue / Forecasted Revenue) × 100**

Target accuracy: ±10% variance for commit forecast

## 2. Commercial Approval Rules

### 2.1 Approval Thresholds
All sales transactions require appropriate commercial approvals based on deal size and type:

#### 2.1.1 Individual Contributor Approval
- Sales Representative: Up to $25,000 ACV
- Requirements: Standard terms, no customizations, approved pricing

#### 2.1.2 Sales Manager Approval
- Sales Manager: $25,001 - $100,000 ACV
- Requirements: Standard terms, approved pricing, minimal customizations

#### 2.1.3 CSO Approval
- CSO: $100,001 - $250,000 ACV
- Requirements: May include standard customizations, non-standard terms requiring review

#### 2.1.4 CEO Approval
- CEO: Over $250,000 ACV or strategic significance
- Requirements: All deals exceeding threshold, strategic partnerships, enterprise agreements

### 2.2 Approval Process Requirements
All commercial approvals require:

#### 2.2.1 Documentation Requirements
- Complete opportunity record in CRM
- Approved proposal document attached
- Pricing worksheet with calculations
- Customer commitment evidence (emails, meeting notes)
- Risk assessment and mitigation plan

#### 2.2.2 Approval Workflow
1. Sales Representative prepares approval package
2. Sales Manager reviews and provides initial approval (if within authority)
3. Package forwarded to next approver if exceeding current authority level
4. Approver reviews and provides decision within 2 business days
5. Approved package returned to Sales Representative for execution
6. All approvals documented in CRM opportunity record

#### 2.2.3 Special Approval Types
- **Pricing Exceptions:** Require Finance approval regardless of deal size
- **Legal/Non-standard Terms:** Require Legal department review
- **Custom Development:** Require Product and Technology approval
- **Multi-year Agreements:** Require Finance approval for terms beyond 12 months

### 2.3 Pricing Governance
Standard pricing guidelines that govern all transactions:

#### 2.3.1 Discount Authority
- Sales Representative: Up to 10% discount (with manager notification)
- Sales Manager: 11-20% discount (requires justification)
- CSO: 21-35% discount (requires business case)
- CEO: Over 35% discount (requires strategic justification)

#### 2.3.2 Price Waterfall Elements
All pricing must consider:
- List Price (baseline)
- Volume Discounts (based on quantity/term)
- Promotional Discounts (time-limited offers)
- Contractual Discounts (multi-year commitments)
- Special Pricing Approvals (exceptions requiring escalation)

#### 2.3.3 Pricing Exceptions Process
Non-standard pricing requires:
1. Business case documentation (volume, strategic value, competitive situation)
2. Impact analysis on margins and quotas
3. Approval chain based on discount percentage
4. Documentation in CRM with expiration date (if applicable)

## 3. Commercial Governance Standards

### 3.1 Deal Desk Function
For complex transactions, a Deal Desk may be convened:

- **Purpose:** Provide cross-functional review for non-standard or complex deals
- **Composition:** CSO, Finance Lead, Legal Lead, Product Lead (as needed)
- **Trigger:** Deals exceeding $150,000 ACV, non-standard terms, or strategic significance
- **Timeline:** Review completed within 3 business days of request

### 3.2 Contract Governance
All contracts must adhere to:

- **Standard Template:** Use approved check contract template
- **Term Limits:** Maximum 36 months without special approval
- **Payment Terms:** Net 30 days standard; deviations require Finance approval
- **Auto-renewal:** Requires explicit customer consent and 60-day notice period
- **Termination Clauses:** Standard mutual termination for convenience with 90-day notice

### 3.3 Revenue Recognition Considerations
Sales must ensure transactions meet revenue recognition criteria:

- **Persuasive Evidence:** Signed contract or purchase order
- **Delivery:** Solution delivered or service performed
- **Fixed Fee:** Price is determinable and not contingent
- **Collectibility:** Reasonable assurance of payment collection

All opportunities must address these criteria before Stage 5 progression.
