## 1. Department Mandate

Sales owns revenue conversion through disciplined pipeline management, commercial execution, and controlled deal flow. We turn market interest into signed business.

## 2. Business Role

Sales is the engine that converts qualified demand into revenue for {checklist}'s simple, actionable solutions. We ensure market interest generated by marketing is properly qualified, progressed through our sales process, and closed as revenue.

## 3. Position in Company Value Chain

Sales sits between **Go-to-Market Execution** and **Customer Delivery** in the company value chain:

1. Market insight → Marketing (positioning, messaging, demand generation) → **Sales (qualification, progression, closing)** → Customer delivery → Client usage and outcome → Feedback and iteration

## 4. Core Responsibilities

### 4.1 What We Own
- Sales process and pipeline structure
- Qualification standards and opportunity management
- Forecast discipline and deal progression
- Proposal process and commercial handoff into onboarding
- Revenue acquisition reporting
- Sales workflow discipline

### 4.2 What We Do NOT Own
- Marketing strategy
- Product roadmap
- Technical implementation
- Delivery operations
- Financial policy

## 5. Success Metrics

Sales success is measured by:
- Revenue booked vs. target
- Pipeline coverage ratio
- Win rate
- Sales cycle length
- Average deal size
- Forecast accuracy
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
- Land-and-expand metrics

## 6. Escalation Path

- **Normal:** CSO → CEO  
- **Urgent:** Immediate escalation to CEO via approved communication channels  
- **Cross-functional issues:** Escalate to CEO who convenes ExCom  

## 7. Operating Standards

All sales work follows company-wide operating rules. Sales departmental standards are documented in the Sales Operational Playbook section.

## 8. Required Deliverables

Sales produces the following recurring deliverables:
- Qualification criteria and sales stage definitions
- Pipeline rules and proposal workflow
- Forecast method and commercial approval rules
- Handoff standard to CCO
- Objection handling library
- Win-loss review process
- Weekly sales report
- Sales Definition of Done library

## 9. Dependencies

Sales depends on:
- **Marketing:** For qualified lead generation and market insights
- **Product:** For product information, features, and roadmap insights
- **Technology:** For demonstration environments and technical validation
- **Customer Success:** For smooth handoff and expansion opportunities
- **Finance:** For pricing approval and contract terms
- **CEO:** For strategic direction and approval of major deals

## 10. Review Cadence

- Weekly: Pipeline review with CEO
- Monthly: Forecast review with ExCom
- Quarterly: Sales strategy and planning review
- Annually: Compensation plan review and quota setting
