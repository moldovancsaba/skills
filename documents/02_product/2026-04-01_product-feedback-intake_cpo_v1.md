## Feedback Channels

| Channel | Owner | Frequency |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Customer support tickets | Customer | Continuous |
| Sales team input | Sales | Weekly |
| Direct customer calls | Customer | As needed |
| Market research | Marketing | Quarterly |

## Intake Workflow

1. **Collection** — All feedback logged in feedback tracking system
2. **Categorisation** — Tagged by type (bug, feature, improvement)
3. **Initial Triage** — Product team reviews within 48 hours
4. **Prioritisation** — Apply prioritisation framework
5. **Roadmap Integration** — Approved items added to backlog/roadmap
6. **Feedback Loop** — Customer informed of outcome where appropriate

## Triage Criteria

| Criteria | Questions |
|----------|-----------|
| Frequency | How many customers report this? |
| Severity | Is this blocking work? |
| Value | What is the business impact? |
| Feasibility | Can we build this? |

## Response Standards

| Priority | Response Time |
|----------|---------------|
| Critical | 24 hours |
| High | 1 week |
| Medium | 2 weeks |
| Low | Next roadmap review |

## Feedback Review Meeting

Bi-weekly product review meeting to:
- Review new feedback
- Re-prioritise backlog
- Update roadmap
