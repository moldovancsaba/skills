## 1. Customer Department Value Chain

The Customer function owns everything from signed contract to retained, satisfied client.

```
Signed Contract → Onboarding → Delivery → Support → Feedback → Retention
```

### Value Chain Stages

| Stage | Input | Output | Owner |
|-------|-------|--------|-------|
| Signed Contract | Executed agreement, client details | Handoff confirmation to Customer | CCO |
| Onboarding | Signed contract, client info | Active client account, kickoff complete | CCO |
| Delivery | Onboarded client, defined scope | Delivered output, client confirmation | CCO |
| Support | Active client, issue or request | Resolved issue, logged communication | CCO |
| Feedback | Completed delivery or support cycle | Captured feedback, routed insights | CCO |
| Retention | Ongoing relationship | Renewed engagement, no preventable churn | CCO |

### Stage Gates

- **Contract → Onboarding:** All client details received, account created, kickoff scheduled.
- **Onboarding → Delivery:** Kickoff complete, expectations aligned, delivery plan confirmed by client.
- **Delivery → Support:** Deliverable handed to client, client acknowledged receipt, support window opened.
- **Support → Feedback:** Issue resolved or delivery completed, feedback requested and captured.
- **Feedback → Retention:** Feedback reviewed, action items logged, retention risk assessed.

## 4. Compliance and Reporting

### Weekly Compliance Audit
The CCO audits the following weekly:
- All active client accounts have current status documented
- All deliveries have logged communication
- All support issues have resolution or escalation path documented
- All feedback is captured and routed
- No client communication is undocumented
- All checklists are followed and completed

### Material Risk Reporting
The CCO reports to the CEO immediately when:
- A client relationship is at material risk of loss
- A delivery failure has occurred or is imminent
- A systemic support issue is identified
- Retention metrics show material decline
- Any issue requires cross-departmental intervention beyond CCO authority

## 5. Cross-Department Routing

| Signal From | Routes To | Trigger |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Client requests new feature | Product | Feature request captured in feedback |
| Client reports system issue | Technology | Bug or system failure identified |
| Client escalates billing | Operations | Billing dispute or policy question |
| Client requests scope change | Product + Operations | Scope change affects delivery or cost |
| Client satisfaction drops below threshold | CEO | Retention risk identified |
