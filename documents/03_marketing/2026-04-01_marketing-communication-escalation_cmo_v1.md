## 1. Communication Flow

### 1.1 Internal Marketing Communication
- **Daily:** Stand-up meetings (15 mins) for active campaigns
- **Weekly:** Team sync (1 hour) for planning and review
- **Monthly:** Strategic review (2 hours) with marketing team
- **Quarterly:** Planning session (half day) with ExCom

### 1.2 Cross-Functional Communication
- **Sales:** Weekly lead quality review, monthly pipeline alignment
- **Product:** Bi-weekly roadmap sync, ad-hoc for launches
- **Customer Success:** Monthly customer insights review
- **Technology:** Weekly tech stack review (as needed)
- **Finance:** Monthly budget review, quarterly planning
- **CEO:** Weekly performance review, ad-hoc escalations

## 2. Escalation Rules

### 2.1 Escalation Triggers
Escalate to CEO when:
- Lead quality drops below threshold for 2 consecutive weeks
- Campaign ROI is negative for 2 consecutive months
- Brand violation occurs
- Budget overrun exceeds 10%
- Cross-functional conflict unresolved within 48 hours
- Legal/compliance issue identified
- Market crisis or PR issue

### 2.2 Escalation Path
1. **First escalation:** CMO → CEO (written communication)
2. **Urgent escalation:** CMO → CEO (immediate call/message)
3. **Critical escalation:** CMO → CEO → ExCom (emergency meeting)

### 2.3 Escalation Documentation
All escalations must include:
- Issue description
- Impact assessment
- Timeline
- Attempted solutions
- Recommended action
- Required decision/assistance

## 3. Communication Standards

### 3.1 Meeting Standards
- All meetings have agendas sent 24 hours in advance
- All meetings have written notes/decisions documented within 24 hours
- All meetings end with clear action items and owners
- Recurring meetings reviewed quarterly for necessity

### 3.2 Written Communication
- Use company communication template for formal updates
- Subject lines must be descriptive and include project name
- Critical decisions documented in official documents, not email
- Email is for coordination, not decision-making

### 3.3 Decision Documentation
- All material decisions documented in official documents
- Decision log maintained for marketing department
- Decisions include rationale, alternatives considered, and impact

## 4. Collaboration Tools

### 4.1 Approved Tools
- **Documentation:** /Users/Shared/Projects/checklist/documents
- **Communication:** Company-approved messaging platform
- **Project Management:** Company-approved project management tool
- **File Storage:** Company-approved cloud storage

### 4.2 Tool Usage Rules
- No unofficial storage of marketing assets
- All marketing documents follow naming convention
- Version control maintained for all assets
- Access permissions managed by CMO

## 5. Reporting Communication

### 5.1 Regular Reports
- **Weekly:** Marketing performance report to CEO
- **Monthly:** Campaign performance reports to ExCom
- **Quarterly:** Marketing strategy review to ExCom
- **Annually:** Marketing plan to CEO

### 5.2 Ad-hoc Reports
- Campaign post-mortems within 1 week of completion
- Incident reports within 24 hours of identification
- Competitive analysis reports as needed

## 6. External Communication

### 6.1 Brand Voice
- All external communication follows brand guidelines
- Social media posts approved by CMO or delegate
- Press releases approved by CEO
- Customer communication approved by CMO and Customer Success

### 6.2 Spokesperson Designation
- **Primary:** CMO
- **Secondary:** CEO
- **Product:** CPO
- **Technical:** CTO
- **Customer:** CCO

## 7. Communication Compliance

- Weekly audit of communication standards
- Non-compliance escalated to CEO
- Training provided for new team members
- Annual review of communication standards
