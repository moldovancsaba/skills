# Checklist: Escalation

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure material issues are escalated to the correct owner within required timeframes
**Cadence:** Per escalation event
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Identify escalation trigger (severity, impact, or policy breach)
- [ ] Determine correct escalation target per Escalation Matrix
- [ ] Prepare escalation summary: issue, impact, actions taken, what is needed
- [ ] Escalate to correct owner within required timeframe
- [ ] Log escalation in customer record
- [ ] Inform customer of escalation and expected timeline
- [ ] Track escalation until resolution
- [ ] Confirm resolution with customer
- [ ] Document lessons learned
- [ ] Close escalation record

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked and the Definition of Done is satisfied.

### Definition of Done

- Issue escalated to correct owner within required timeframe
- Customer is informed of escalation status
- Escalation is fully logged
- Resolution is confirmed and documented

### Storage

Completed checklists stored in customer account record under 04_customer/
