## 1. Report Purpose and Audience

The Weekly Sales Report provides:
- Pipeline health and forecast accuracy visibility
- Sales performance tracking against targets
- Early warning of risks and opportunities
- Basis for sales coaching and resource allocation
- Executive summary for CEO and leadership team

**Primary Audience:** CEO, CSO, Sales Leadership  
**Secondary Audience:** ExCom, Finance, Product, Marketing (as needed)

## 2. Report Schedule and Delivery

- **Frequency:** Weekly, delivered every Monday by 9:00 AM local time
- **Format:** Standardized document or dashboard (as specified)
- **Distribution:** Email to designated recipients, posted to team repository
- **Preparation:** Compiled by Sales Operations or designated sales representative
- **Review:** Reviewed by CSO before distribution

## 3. Report Sections

### 3.1 Executive Summary
High-level overview of sales performance:
- Weekly booking total vs. target
- Pipeline status and forecast accuracy
- Key wins and losses from previous week
- Immediate risks requiring attention
- Notable trends or observations

### 3.2 Booking Performance
Detailed revenue performance metrics:
- **Current Period:** Week-to-date, month-to-date, quarter-to-date
- **Bookings:** Actual vs. target (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- **Average Deal Size:** Current period and trending
- **Deal Count:** Number of closed won opportunities
- **Win Rate:** Percentage of opportunities won vs. lost
- **Sales Cycle Length:** Average days from creation to close

### 3.3 Pipeline Health
Comprehensive pipeline assessment:
- **Total Pipeline Value:** Sum of all opportunity values
- **Weighted Pipeline:** Pipeline value × stage probability
- **Pipeline by Stage:** Distribution across sales stages
- **Pipeline Coverage Ratio:** Current pipeline vs. quota target
- **Stage Conversion Rates:** Movement efficiency between stages
- **Pipeline Velocity:** Average time opportunities spend in each stage
- **New Opportunities:** Count and value added this week
- **Advanced Opportunities:** Count and value moved to later stages
- **Stalled Opportunities:** Count and value with no activity >14 days

### 3.4 Forecast Accuracy
Forecast performance metrics:
- **Commit Forecast:** Current commit forecast vs. target
- **Best Case Forecast:** Best case forecast vs. target
- **Forecast Accuracy:** Variance between forecast and actual (rolling 4-week)
- **Forecast Category Attainment:** % of forecast in each category (Pipeline, Commit, Upside, Closed)
- **Forecast Changes:** Week-over-week changes to forecast amounts

### 3.5 Activity Metrics
Sales activity and productivity indicators:
- **Outreach Volume:** Calls, emails, LinkedIn touches per rep
- **Meeting Conversion:** Ratio of outreach to meetings booked
- **Demo/Validation Sessions:** Technical validations conducted
- **Proposal Output:** Proposals delivered and pending
- **Pipeline Meetings:** Customer-facing sales meetings conducted
- **Activity Quota Attainment:** % of reps meeting activity targets

### 3.6 Risk and Opportunity Identification
Flagged items requiring attention:
- **At-Risk Deals:** Opportunities with concerning indicators
- **Slippage Risks:** Deals likely to miss forecast period
- **Unexpected Opportunities:** New high-potential developments
- **Competitive Intelligence:** Significant competitive movements
- **Resource Constraints:** Capacity or skill gaps affecting performance
- **Process Bottlenecks:** Stages where deals consistently stall

### 3.7 Individual Performance
Representative-level metrics (aggregated or blinded as appropriate):
- **Booking Attainment:** Individual vs. target
- **Pipeline Generation:** New opportunity creation
- **Activity Levels:** Outbound and customer-facing activities
- **Conversion Rates:** Lead to opportunity, opportunity to close
- **Average Deal Size:** Individual performance vs. team average
- **Forecast Accuracy:** Individual forecast prediction accuracy

### 3.8 Product and Market Insights
Feedback from field interactions:
- **Product Feedback:** Client requests, gaps, or suggestions
- **Competitive Intelligence:** Competitor activities or offerings
- **Market Trends:** Emerging needs or shifting priorities
- **Pricing Feedback:** Client reactions to pricing or packaging
- **Messaging Effectiveness:** Resonance of value propositions

## 4. Data Sources and Validation

The Weekly Sales Report draws from:
- **CRM System:** Primary source for opportunity and activity data
- **Sales Activity Logs:** Call logs, email tracking, meeting records
- **Forecasting Tool:** Commitment and forecast submissions
- **Financial System:** Booked revenue and invoicing data
- **Marketing Systems:** Lead source and campaign performance
- **Product Usage:** Adoption and utilization data (where available)

Data validation requirements:
- CRM data must be updated by Friday EOD for inclusion in Monday report
- Forecasts must be submitted by Monday 9:00 AM cutoff
- Discrepancies between systems must be investigated and resolved
- Estimates or projections must be clearly labeled as such

## 5. Report Format Standards

### 5.1 Visual Elements
- Use consistent color coding (green = on track, yellow = caution, red = requires action)
- Include trend arrows showing week-over-week movement
- Apply conditional formatting to highlight threshold breaches
- Use sparklines or mini-charts for trend visualization
- Maintain consistent branding and formatting

### 5.2 Narrative Components
- Keep executive summary to 3-5 bullet points maximum
- Provide context for significant variances or changes
- Focus on insights, not just data presentation
- Clearly separate facts from interpretation or opinion
- Include action items with owners and due dates where applicable

### 5.3 Length and Readability
- Target length: 2-3 pages (excluding detailed appendices)
- Use clear headings and subheadings for easy scanning
- Define all acronyms on first use
- Use plain language avoiding jargon where possible
- Include glossary of terms as appendix if needed

## 6. Report Preparation Process

### 6.1 Data Collection (Friday EOD - Sunday)
- Extract CRM data as of Friday close
- Gather forecast submissions from sales team
- Compile activity metrics from sales enablement tools
- Collect financial booking data from finance system
- Gather qualitative inputs from sales managers

### 6.2 Analysis and Compilation (Monday Morning)
- Validate data completeness and accuracy
- Calculate metrics and compare to targets
- Identify trends, outliers, and significant changes
- Prepare narrative insights and explanations
- Format report according to template standards

### 6.3 Review and Distribution (Monday by 9:00 AM)
- CSO review for accuracy and completeness
- Incorporate any feedback or corrections
- Finalize report and distribute to recipients
- Archive report in team repository with standard naming:
  `YYYY-WW_WentSalesReport_Version.md`

## 7. Escalation and Action Triggers

The report should trigger specific actions when:
- **Booking Attainment:** <80% of weekly target for 2 consecutive weeks
- **Pipeline Coverage:** <2.0x quota coverage
- **Forecast Accuracy:** >15% variance for 2 consecutive weeks
- **Stage Velocity:** Opportunities stagnant in stage >30 days
- **Win Rate:** <20% win rate for individual or team
- **Activity Levels:** <70% of activity quota for individual
- **Slippage Risk:** >20% of commit forecast at risk
