## 1. Target Audience Overview

{checklist} delivers simple, actionable solutions to clients. Our audience values clarity, speed, and reliability over complexity.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### 2.1 Firmographic Profile

| Attribute | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Company Size** | 1-50 employees |
| **Industry** | Professional services, consulting, small agencies |
| **Revenue** | Under $10M annually |
| **Location** | Global, English-speaking markets |
| **Tech Maturity** | Uses basic SaaS tools, not enterprise systems |

### 2.2 Role-Based Profile

| Attribute | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Decision Maker** | CEO, Founder, or department head |
| **Champion** | Operations manager, team lead, or senior consultant |
| **End User** | Individual contributors, project managers |
| **Influencer** | Advisors, board members, mentors |

### 2.3 Psychographic Profile

| Attribute | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Pain Point** | Overwhelmed by complexity, lack of systems |
| **Aspiration** | Simple, repeatable processes that scale |
| **Decision Driver** | Time savings, clarity, accountability |
| **Objection** | "We tried systems before; they were too complex" |

## 3. Anti-ICP (Who We Do NOT Target)

| Attribute | Reason to Exclude |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Enterprise companies (500+ employees) | Complexity mismatch |
| Non-English speaking markets | Language barrier |
| Highly regulated industries | Compliance overhead |
| Companies without a decision maker | No clear buyer |

## 4. Validation Criteria

The ICP is considered valid when:

- At least 3 customer interviews confirm pain points
- Sales confirms these attributes match closed-won deals
- Marketing campaigns targeting this profile achieve >2% conversion
- Product roadmap aligns with these user needs
