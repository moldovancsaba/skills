# Checklist: Renewal Support

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure every customer renewal is prepared and supported systematically
**Cadence:** 60 days before each renewal date
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Confirm renewal date and commercial terms
- [ ] Review account health (delivery history, issue log, feedback)
- [ ] Prepare renewal readiness report
- [ ] Identify any outstanding issues and resolve or document
- [ ] Compile feedback summary
- [ ] Inform customer of renewal timeline
- [ ] Schedule renewal conversation
- [ ] Conduct renewal conversation
- [ ] Document renewal outcome
- [ ] If renewing: prepare handoff to active delivery
- [ ] If not renewing: execute exit process
- [ ] Notify CEO of renewal status and any risks

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked and the Definition of Done is satisfied.

### Definition of Done

- Account health review is complete
- Renewal readiness report is delivered
- Customer is engaged on renewal timeline
- Outstanding issues are resolved or documented
- Renewal conversation is scheduled or outcome is documented
- CEO is informed of renewal status and any risks

### Storage

Completed checklists stored in customer account record under 04_customer/
