## CEO Instruction to Executive Committee

Each executive — CMO, CSO, CPO, CTO, CCO, CFO — must submit a departmental pack with the following sections:

1. department purpose and business role
2. departmental value chain
3. workflow and pipeline stages
4. communication flow and escalation rules
5. documentation requirements
6. Definitions of Done for major recurring work
7. checklist library
8. role ownership map
9. reporting cadence
10. cross-functional handoff rules

## Submission Format

- All documents follow the company naming standard: YYYY-MM-DD_topic_owner_version.md
- All documents include the required document fields (see Documentation Architecture)
- All checklists use the checklist template from /08_templates/

## Deadline

Executive playbook sections are due within 7 days of agent activation.

## Enforcement

The CEO will review submissions, approve Playbook V1, and move to formal enforcement. Non-compliance will be escalated and corrected immediately.
