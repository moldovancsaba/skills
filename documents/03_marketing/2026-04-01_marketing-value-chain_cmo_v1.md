## 1. Overview

The marketing value chain transforms market understanding into qualified leads for Sales. Each stage adds value and moves prospects closer to conversion.

## 2. Value Chain Stages

### Stage 1: Market Insight & Audience Research
**Input:** Market data, competitor analysis, customer feedback
**Process:** Research, analysis, segmentation
**Output:** Audience definitions, ICP, market trends report
**Owner:** CMO / Marketing Analyst
**Handoff to:** Positioning & Messaging

### Stage 2: Positioning & Messaging
**Input:** Audience definitions, market insights
**Process:** Positioning framework, messaging development
**Output:** Positioning document, messaging playbook
**Owner:** CMO / Content Strategist
**Handoff to:** Campaign Planning

### Stage 3: Campaign Planning & Strategy
**Input:** Positioning, messaging, business objectives
**Process:** Campaign ideation, channel selection, budget allocation
**Output:** Campaign plan, content calendar, channel strategy
**Owner:** CMO / Campaign Manager
**Handoff to:** Content Production

### Stage 4: Content Production & Asset Creation
**Input:** Campaign plan, messaging, brand guidelines
**Process:** Content creation, design, production
**Output:** Marketing assets (blog posts, social media, ads, videos, etc.)
**Owner:** Content Team / Design Team
**Handoff to:** Distribution & Amplification

### Stage 5: Distribution & Amplification
**Input:** Marketing assets, channel strategy
**Process:** Publishing, promotion, amplification
**Output:** Live campaigns, content distribution
**Owner:** Channel Owners / Social Media Manager
**Handoff to:** Lead Capture & Nurturing

### Stage 6: Lead Capture & Nurturing
**Input:** Campaign traffic, engagement data
**Process:** Lead capture, scoring, nurturing
**Output:** Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)
**Owner:** Demand Generation / Marketing Ops
**Handoff to:** Sales Handoff

### Stage 7: Sales Handoff & Reporting
**Input:** MQLs, campaign performance data
**Process:** Lead validation, handoff to Sales, reporting
**Output:** Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs), performance reports
**Owner:** CMO / Sales Liaison
**Handoff to:** Sales Team

## 3. Feedback Loops

- **Sales feedback:** Regular feedback from Sales on lead quality
- **Performance data:** Campaign analytics inform future planning
- **Customer insights:** Customer Success provides testimonials and case studies
- **Market signals:** Product team shares roadmap insights

## 4. Value Chain Metrics

| Stage | Key Metrics |
|-------|-------------|
| Market Insight | Market coverage, ICP accuracy |
| Positioning | Message clarity scores, brand consistency |
| Campaign Planning | Plan completeness, budget adherence |
| Content Production | Asset quality, production time |
| Distribution | Reach, engagement rates |
| Lead Capture | Conversion rates, cost per lead |
| Sales Handoff | MQL to SQL conversion, lead quality score |

## 5. Dependencies & Handoffs

- **Product:** Provides product information for positioning
- **Sales:** Provides feedback on lead quality and market signals
- **Technology:** Supports marketing technology stack
- **Customer Success:** Provides customer insights and testimonials
- **Finance:** Approves marketing budget

## 6. Escalation Points

- **Lead quality issues:** CMO escalates to CEO
- **Budget overruns:** CMO escalates to CEO and Finance
- **Cross-functional conflicts:** CEO convenes ExCom
- **Brand violations:** Immediate escalation to CMO
