# Checklist: Feedback Capture

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure all customer feedback is captured, categorized, and routed correctly
**Cadence:** Per feedback event
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Record feedback verbatim with source, date, customer, and context
- [ ] Categorize feedback (product, delivery, support, commercial, strategic)
- [ ] Assess urgency and impact
- [ ] Flag churn risk or material dissatisfaction if present
- [ ] Route to correct team: CPO (product), CSO (commercial), CEO (strategic), or handle internally
- [ ] Inform customer that feedback was received
- [ ] Track feedback item until resolution or acknowledgment by receiving team
- [ ] Log feedback in customer record
- [ ] Flag systemic patterns for review

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked and the Definition of Done is satisfied.

### Definition of Done

- Feedback is captured verbatim with full context
- Feedback is categorized correctly
- Feedback is routed to the correct team or handled internally
- Customer is informed of receipt and any action taken
- Feedback is logged in customer record
- Systemic patterns are flagged for review
