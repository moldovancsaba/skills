## Workflow Stages

### Stage 1: Delivery Planning
**Trigger:** Onboarding complete, customer moved to active status
**Input:** Confirmed scope, delivery schedule, customer preferences
**Output:** Delivery plan with milestones, owners, and dates

**Actions:**
- Map delivery milestones against agreed scope
- Assign delivery owner for each milestone
- Confirm delivery schedule with customer
- Document delivery plan in customer record
- Set up tracking for each milestone

**Stage Gate:** Delivery plan documented and customer-confirmed

### Stage 2: Delivery Preparation
**Trigger:** Milestone approaching (minimum 48 hours before delivery)
**Input:** Delivery plan milestone, prepared deliverable
**Output:** Deliverable ready for transmission, quality-checked

**Actions:**
- Verify deliverable completeness against milestone criteria
- Quality check against product standards
- Prepare delivery communication (see Client Communication Standard)
- Confirm delivery method and timing with customer if needed

**Stage Gate:** Deliverable verified and ready for dispatch

### Stage 3: Delivery Execution
**Trigger:** Scheduled delivery time
**Input:** Verified deliverable, delivery communication
**Output:** Deliverable transmitted to customer, delivery logged

**Actions:**
- Transmit deliverable via agreed channel
- Send delivery notification with clear description
- Include any required instructions or next steps
- Log delivery in customer record with timestamp

**Stage Gate:** Deliverable sent, delivery logged

### Stage 4: Delivery Confirmation
**Trigger:** Delivery sent
**Input:** Delivery log
**Output:** Customer acknowledgment or follow-up action

**Actions:**
- Request delivery confirmation from customer
- If no response within 48 hours, send follow-up
- Document customer acknowledgment or questions
- Address any delivery issues immediately

**Stage Gate:** Customer confirmed receipt or issue is being resolved

### Stage 5: Outcome Verification
**Trigger:** Delivery confirmed
**Input:** Customer acknowledgment, deliverable usage
**Output:** Outcome verified, feedback captured, milestone closed

**Actions:**
- Verify customer is able to use the deliverable as intended
- Capture feedback on delivery quality (see Feedback Capture Standard)
- Document any issues, adjustments, or improvement suggestions
- Close milestone in delivery plan
- Schedule next milestone or check-in

**Stage Gate:** Outcome verified, feedback captured, milestone closed

## Delivery Cadence Rules

- Standard cadence: per agreed client schedule
- Missed delivery: escalate within 4 hours
- Client-requested changes: log, assess impact, confirm with client before execution
- No delivery without quality check against Definition of Done
- Deliverable does not meet quality standard → Hold delivery, escalate to CTO
- Scope change requested → Document, assess, escalate to CSO + CEO for approval

## Definition of Done

Delivery is not done unless:
- Customer received the required output
- Customer confirmed receipt
- Delivery communication is logged
- Current status is clear
- Next milestone or action is named
- Unresolved issues are documented
- Feedback is captured where relevant
- Product implications are routed to Product
- Delivery or quality implications are routed to Technology
