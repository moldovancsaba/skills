**Purpose:** Define completion standards for all major recurring customer work types
**Status:** Active
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Relevant Team:** Customer
**Source of Request:** CCO Mandate
**Linked Workflow:** Customer Value Chain
**Definition of Done:** This document defines the standards themselves
**Related Dependencies:** Company Definition of Done (00_company)

---

## 1. Onboarding DoD

Onboarding is not done unless:
- Customer received and acknowledged welcome materials
- Account is fully configured and tested
- Communication cadence is established and confirmed
- Delivery path is operational
- All onboarding steps are documented
- Customer is transitioned to active delivery status
- Initial feedback is captured
- Onboarding record is closed and archived

## 2. Delivery DoD

Delivery is not done unless:
- Customer received the required output
- Customer confirmed receipt
- Delivery communication is logged
- Current status is clear
- Next milestone or action is named
- Unresolved issues are documented
- Feedback is captured where relevant
- Product implications are routed to Product
- Delivery or quality implications are routed to Technology

## 3. Issue Resolution DoD

Issue resolution is not done unless:
- Root cause is identified or escalation path is documented
- Resolution is confirmed with customer
- All communications are logged
- Issue record is complete with resolution summary
- Feedback on support experience is captured
- Systemic issues are flagged for review
- Product or technology implications are routed to the correct team

## 4. Client Communication DoD

Client communication is not done unless:
- Message follows the required structure
- Communication is logged in customer record
- Customer received the communication
- Any required action or next step is clear
- Follow-up is scheduled where needed

## 5. Client Reporting DoD

Client reporting is not done unless:
- Report data is verified and accurate
- Report follows approved template and standard
- Report is delivered to customer
- Delivery is logged in customer record
- Customer acknowledgment or response is tracked
- Report is archived in correct location
- Any action items are identified and assigned

## 6. Feedback Handling DoD

Feedback handling is not done unless:
- Feedback is captured verbatim with full context
- Feedback is categorized correctly
- Feedback is routed to the correct team or handled internally
- Customer is informed of receipt and any action taken
- Feedback is logged in customer record
- Systemic patterns are flagged for review

## 7. Account Transition DoD

Account transition is not done unless:
- All checklist items are completed
- Receiving party has confirmed acceptance
- Customer is informed of the transition
- All documentation is updated and stored correctly
- No open items are left unassigned

## 8. Renewal Support DoD

Renewal support is not done unless:
- Account health review is complete
- Renewal readiness report is delivered
- Customer is engaged on renewal timeline
- Outstanding issues are resolved or documented
- Renewal conversation is scheduled or outcome is documented
- CEO is informed of renewal status and any risks

---

## Universal Customer DoD

In addition to the specific DoD above, all customer work must satisfy the company-wide Definition of Done (00_company/2026-04-01_definition-of-done_ceo_v1.md).
