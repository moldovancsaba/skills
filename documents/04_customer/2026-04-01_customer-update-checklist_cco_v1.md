# Checklist: Customer Update

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure every customer status update follows the communication standard
**Cadence:** Per update (weekly, milestone, or as needed)
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Gather current status data from customer record
- [ ] Draft update following message structure standard
- [ ] Include: what changed, why it matters, what is needed, next owner, deadline
- [ ] Review for accuracy and completeness
- [ ] Send update via agreed channel
- [ ] Log communication in customer record
- [ ] Track customer response or acknowledgment
- [ ] Address any questions or concerns raised
- [ ] Schedule next update if needed

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked and the Definition of Done is satisfied.

### Definition of Done

- Message follows the required structure
- Communication is logged in customer record
- Customer received the communication
- Any required action or next step is clear
- Follow-up is scheduled where needed

### Storage

Completed checklists stored in customer account record under 04_customer/
