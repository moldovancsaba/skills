## Workflow Stages

### Stage 1: Receive Handoff (Post-Sale)
**Trigger:** CSO marks deal closed-won and submits handoff package
**Input:** Closed-won notification + handoff package from Sales
**Output:** Onboarding ticket created, account record established

**Actions:**
- Verify handoff package completeness (see Account Transition Checklist)
- Create onboarding record with customer details, scope, and timeline
- Assign onboarding owner
- Confirm start date with customer within 24 hours of handoff receipt

**Stage Gate:** Handoff package verified and onboarding ticket created

### Stage 2: Kickoff Preparation
**Trigger:** Onboarding ticket assigned
**Input:** Customer details, scope, handoff notes
**Output:** Kickoff agenda, welcome materials, account setup plan

**Actions:**
- Review customer profile and agreed scope
- Prepare welcome package and onboarding schedule
- Draft kickoff meeting agenda
- Set up internal account record and communication log
- Confirm kickoff date and time with customer

**Stage Gate:** Kickoff scheduled and materials prepared

### Stage 3: Kickoff Execution
**Trigger:** Scheduled kickoff meeting
**Input:** Kickoff agenda, welcome package
**Output:** Completed kickoff, confirmed expectations, documented agreements

**Actions:**
- Conduct kickoff meeting (or send async equivalent)
- Introduce process, communication cadence, and escalation path
- Confirm scope, timeline, and success criteria with customer
- Document any scope clarifications or adjustments
- Share communication standard with customer

**Stage Gate:** Kickoff completed, agreements documented, customer confirmed understanding

### Stage 4: Account Setup
**Trigger:** Kickoff completed
**Input:** Confirmed scope, customer details
**Output:** Fully configured account, delivery path established

**Actions:**
- Complete all account configuration per scope
- Verify delivery mechanisms are operational
- Confirm customer access and permissions
- Test delivery path end-to-end
- Document setup completion

**Stage Gate:** Account fully configured and tested

### Stage 5: Onboarding Close
**Trigger:** Account setup complete
**Input:** Setup confirmation, customer acknowledgment
**Output:** Onboarding complete, customer moved to active delivery

**Actions:**
- Send onboarding completion confirmation to customer
- Log all onboarding communications and decisions
- Transition customer to active delivery status
- Schedule first delivery milestone or check-in
- Capture initial feedback (see Feedback Capture Standard)

**Stage Gate:** Customer confirmed receipt and readiness, onboarding record closed

---

## Stage Gate Rules

- No stage may be skipped
- Each stage must have a documented owner
- Stage completion requires verification against checklist
- Blocked stages must be escalated within 24 hours

## Escalation

- Missing handoff package → Escalate to CSO
- Scope discrepancy between sales agreement and handoff → Escalate to CSO + CCO
- Technical setup failure → Escalate to CTO
- Customer unresponsive for 48+ hours → Escalate to CEO

## Definition of Done

Onboarding is not done unless:
- Customer received and acknowledged welcome materials
- Account is fully configured and tested
- Communication cadence is established and confirmed
- Delivery path is operational
- All onboarding steps are documented
- Customer is transitioned to active delivery status
- Initial feedback is captured
- Onboarding record is closed and archived
