## 1. Purpose and Objectives

The win-loss review process aims to:
- Identify patterns in successful sales approaches
- Understand reasons for lost opportunities
- Improve sales methodology and objection handling
- Inform product development and positioning
- Enhance competitive intelligence
- Increase forecast accuracy

## 2. Review Triggers

Win-loss reviews are conducted for:
- All closed-won opportunities over $25,000 ACV
- All closed-lost opportunities where we reached Stage 4 (Proposal & Validation) or higher
- Any opportunity where client specifically requested feedback
- Quarterly sampling of smaller deals for trend analysis
- Special requests from CSO, CEO, or product team

## 3. Review Timing

Reviews should be conducted:
- Within 10 business days of opportunity closure (won or lost)
- Before details fade from client and sales team memory
- Concurrently with forecast update cycles when possible
- Immediately for strategic losses or unexpected wins

## 4. Review Participants

### 4.1 Core Review Team
- Sales Representative (primary owner of opportunity)
- Sales Manager (direct supervisor)
- CSO or designated sales leader (for strategic deals)
- Solution Engineer/Technical Advisor (if involved in sales process)

### 4.2 Optional Participants
- Marketing Representative (for messaging/positioning insights)
- Product Representative (for product-fit feedback)
- Customer Success Representative (for won deals - early handoff perspective)
- Finance Representative (for pricing-related analysis)

## 5. Review Process

### 5.1 Preparation Phase
- Sales Representative completes pre-review questionnaire
- Gather all relevant opportunity documentation:
  - CRM records and activity history
  - Proposal and presentation materials
  - Email correspondence and meeting notes
  - Competitive intelligence gathered
  - Internal approvals and deal desk notes (if applicable)
- Schedule review meeting (60-90 minutes recommended)

### 5.2 Client Interview (When Possible and Appropriate)
For opportunities where client feedback can be obtained:
- Request 15-20 minute feedback call
- Use standardized interview guide
- Focus on learning, not re-selling or defending loss
- Thank client for their time and consideration
- Document feedback objectively

### 5.3 Internal Review Meeting
Structured meeting following this agenda:

#### 5.3.1 Opportunity Overview (10 minutes)
- Basic facts: client, deal size, timeline, outcome
- Initial expectations vs. actual result
- Key turning points in sales process

#### 5.3.2 Decision Factors Analysis (20 minutes)
- What influenced the final decision?
- Ranking of decision criteria (price, features, relationship, etc.)
- Competitive factors (if applicable)
- Internal client dynamics and politics

#### 5.3.3 Sales Process Evaluation (20 minutes)
- Effectiveness of qualification and discovery
- Quality of solution design and proposal
- Response to objections and concerns
- Timing and pacing of sales activities
- Team coordination and resource utilization

#### 5.3.4 Lessons Learned Identification (15 minutes)
- What did we do well?
- What could we improve?
- What surprised us about the outcome?
- What would we do differently in hindsight?

#### 5.3.5 Action Items Definition (10 minutes)
- Specific improvements to sales approach
- Knowledge to share with team
- Product or messaging feedback to convey
- Follow-up actions with client (if appropriate)

### 5.4 Documentation and Distribution
- Complete Win-Loss Review Form (template in Section 6)
- Store in centralized repository with consistent naming:
  `YYYY-MM-DD_WinLoss_ClientName_Outcome_Version.md`
- Distribute to:
  - Sales team (for learning)
  - Product team (for product feedback)
  - Marketing team (for messaging insights)
  - CSO office (for trend analysis)
- Add key insights to objection handling library or battle cards as appropriate

## 7. Analysis and Reporting

### 7.1 Quarterly Win-Loss Analysis
The CSO office conducts quarterly analysis to identify:
- Win rate trends by segment, product, sales rep
- Common reasons for wins and losses
- Competitive threat assessment
- Product-market fit indicators
- Sales process effectiveness metrics

### 7.2 Metrics Tracked
- Overall win rate (won vs. lost opportunities)
- Win rate by stage at loss
- Win rate by deal size
- Win rate by product/solution type
- Win rate by sales representative/team
- Average sales cycle length for wins vs. losses
- Most common reasons for loss
- Most frequently cited strengths in wins

### 7.3 Reporting Cadence
- Monthly: Win/loss summary in sales pipeline report
- Quarterly: Detailed win-loss analysis presented to ExCom
- Ad-hoc: Special reports for significant trends or concerns
- Real-time: Dashboard showing current win/loss trends

## 8. Continuous Improvement

### 8.1 Feedback Loops
Insights from win-loss reviews inform:
- Objection handling library updates
- Battle card and competitive intelligence updates
- Sales methodology refinements
- Product development priorities
- Marketing messaging adjustments
- Pricing and packaging decisions

### 8.2 Knowledge Sharing
- Monthly sales training session featuring win-loss insights
- Quarterly newsletter with top learnings
- Onboarding materials for new sales representatives
- Annual sales kickoff presentation highlighting key trends
