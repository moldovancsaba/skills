## Transition Type: Sales → Customer (Closed-Won Handoff)

### Pre-Handoff (CSO Responsibility, CCO Verification)
- [ ] Signed agreement or contract on file
- [ ] Scope of work clearly defined and documented
- [ ] Commercial terms confirmed (pricing, payment schedule, duration)
- [ ] Key stakeholders identified (customer side and company side)
- [ ] Customer background and context documented
- [ ] Any special conditions or commitments noted
- [ ] Handoff package submitted to CCO

### Handoff Receipt (CCO Responsibility)
- [ ] Handoff package received and reviewed for completeness
- [ ] Missing items flagged and requested from CSO
- [ ] Customer account record created
- [ ] Onboarding owner assigned
- [ ] Onboarding start date confirmed
- [ ] Customer notified of transition and next steps
- [ ] Internal handoff acknowledgment sent to CSO

### Transition Type: Active → Renewal
- [ ] Account health reviewed (delivery history, issue log, feedback)
- [ ] Renewal date confirmed
- [ ] Renewal readiness report prepared
- [ ] Customer informed of renewal timeline
- [ ] Any outstanding issues resolved or documented
- [ ] Feedback summary compiled
- [ ] Renewal conversation scheduled

### Transition Type: Customer Exit (Churn or Completion)
- [ ] Exit reason documented
- [ ] Final deliverables or obligations confirmed
- [ ] Outstanding issues resolved or acknowledged
- [ ] Final feedback captured
- [ ] Account archived
- [ ] Lessons learned documented
- [ ] CEO notified of material churn

## Definition of Done

Account transition is not done unless:
- All checklist items are completed
- Receiving party has confirmed acceptance
- Customer is informed of the transition
- All documentation is updated and stored correctly
- No open items are left unassigned
