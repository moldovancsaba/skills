## 1. Overview

According to the Sales Department Purpose, sales work is not done unless:
- Opportunity stage is current
- Required discovery facts are recorded
- Qualification is explicit
- Next commercial action is named
- Owner is named
- Commitment date is documented where applicable
- Proposal version is stored correctly
- Forecast status is updated
- Handoff package is complete when closed

This library expands on that standard with specific criteria for various sales activities.

## 2. Opportunity Management Definition of Done

An opportunity is considered "done" for a given stage when:

### 2.1 Stage Progression
- [ ] All required activities for current stage completed
- [ ] All exit criteria for current stage met
- [ ] Next stage entry criteria verified
- [ ] Opportunity stage updated in CRM
- [ ] Probability weighting adjusted to new stage
- [ ] Forecast updated to reflect stage change

### 2.2 Activity Completion
- [ ] All customer interactions logged in CRM within 24 hours
- [ ] Meeting notes and key takeaways documented
- [ ] Action items assigned with owners and due dates
- [ ] Follow-up scheduled and calendar invites sent
- [ ] Preparation completed for next customer interaction

### 2.3 Information Requirements
- [ ] All required discovery facts recorded in opportunity record
- [ ] Qualification status explicitly documented (BANT assessment)
- [ ] Stakeholder map completed and updated
- [ ] Decision process and criteria documented
- [ ] Competitive landscape assessed and recorded
- [ ] Solution fit analysis completed and stored

## 3. Proposal Creation Definition of Done

A proposal is considered "done" when:

### 3.1 Content Requirements
- [ ] Executive summary clearly states business problem and proposed solution
- [ ] Solution description matches documented client requirements
- [ ] Implementation plan includes timeline, milestones, and resource requirements
- [ ] Investment summary provides clear pricing breakdown and payment terms
- [ ] Value proposition includes quantified business benefits and ROI
- [ ] Terms and conditions reflect standard {checklist} agreements
- [ ] Acceptance procedure outlines clear next steps for client approval

### 3.2 Internal Review
- [ ] Solution reviewed and approved by sales engineer or technical lead
- [ ] Pricing validated against guidelines and approval thresholds
- [ ] Legal review completed for non-standard terms
- [ ] Manager approval obtained for proposal delivery
- [ ] All reviewer comments addressed and incorporated

### 3.3 Preparation for Delivery
- [ ] Proposal converted to client-facing format (PDF/presentation)
- [ ] Presentation deck created with appropriate talking points
- [ ] Demo environment prepared (if applicable)
- [ ] Rehearsal completed for presentation delivery
- [ ] Backup materials prepared for potential questions

## 4. Forecast Submission Definition of Done

A forecast submission is considered "done" when:

### 4.1 Data Accuracy
- [ ] All opportunity values current and accurate
- [ ] Stage probabilities correctly applied
- [ ] Commitment status accurately reflected
- [ ] Upside opportunities properly identified and justified
- [ ] Closed won opportunities moved to committed forecast
- [ ] Closed lost opportunities removed from forecast

### 4.2 Supporting Information
- [ ] Forecast assumptions documented for significant changes
- [ ] Risks and mitigations identified for commit forecast items
- [ ] Best case opportunities identified with realization likelihood
- [ ] Sales rep confidence level noted for forecast categories
- [ ] Changes from previous forecast explained and justified

### 4.3 Submission Process
- [ ] Forecast submitted by weekly deadline (Monday 9:00 AM)
- [ ] Submission made through approved forecasting tool
- [ ] Confirmation received of successful submission
- [ ] Discrepancies with pipeline report investigated and resolved
- [ ] Manager notified of significant forecast changes (>20% value change)

## 5. Handoff Preparation Definition of Done

Handoff preparation is considered "done" when:

### 5.1 Package Completeness
- [ ] All contractual documentation gathered and organized
- [ ] Solution configuration details fully documented
- [ ] Client contact information verified and complete
- [ ] Business context and success criteria clearly articulated
- [ ] Sales process artifacts compiled and indexed
- [ ] Implementation requirements clearly defined
- [ ] Timeline and milestones established and validated

### 5.2 Quality Assurance
- [ ] Handoff package reviewed for completeness using checklist
- [ ] Missing items identified and resolution plan developed
- [ ] Inconsistencies or gaps addressed before handoff meeting
- [ ] Package organized for easy consumption by Customer Success team
- [ ] Digital and physical copies prepared as needed

### 5.3 Meeting Preparation
- [ ] Handoff meeting scheduled with Customer Success team
- [ ] Presentation materials prepared for handoff meeting
- [ ] Key talking points reviewed and rehearsed
- [ ] Answers prepared for anticipated questions
- [ ] Commitment confirmed from Customer Success attendees

## 6. Closed-Won Opportunity Definition of Done

A closed-won opportunity is considered "done" when:

### 6.1 Contractual Completion
- [ ] Contract fully executed and deposited (if applicable)
- [ ] Opportunity moved to Stage 6: Closed Won in CRM
- [ ] Forecast updated to reflect committed revenue
- [ ] All financial terms confirmed and documented
- [ ] Implementation kickoff date scheduled and confirmed

### 6.2 Handoff Completion
- [ ] Complete handoff package delivered to Customer Success
- [ ] Handoff meeting conducted and documented
- [ ] Customer Success acknowledgment of package receipt
- [ ] Outstanding questions or concerns resolved
- [ ] Sales representative availability confirmed for transition period

### 6.3 Post-Close Activities
- [ ] Opportunity closed in CRM with correct close date and reason
- [ ] All activities logged and final notes added
- [ ] Post-close review completed with sales manager
- [ ] Lessons learned documented and shared
- [ ] Commission and compensation paperwork submitted

## 7. Closed-Lost Opportunity Definition of Done

A closed-lost opportunity is considered "done" when:

### 7.1 Closure Processing
- [ ] Opportunity moved to Stage 6: Closed Lost in CRM
- [ ] Forecast updated to remove opportunity value
- [ ] Loss reason explicitly documented and categorized
- [ ] Competitive intelligence captured (if applicable)
- [ ] Relationship preservation actions defined and assigned

### 7.2 Analysis and Learning
- [ ] Loss analysis conducted using win-loss review framework
- [ ] Key learnings identified and documented
- [ ] Feedback requested from client (when appropriate)
- [ ] Insights added to objection handling library or battle cards
- [ ] Process improvements identified and tracked

### 7.3 Administrative Completion
- [ ] All activities logged and final notes added
- [ ] Opportunity closed with correct close date and reason
- [ ] Post-close review completed with sales manager
- [ ] Follow-up actions scheduled for relationship nurturing
- [ ] Pipeline metrics updated to reflect removal

## 8. Weekly Activities Definition of Done

Weekly sales activities are considered "done" when:

### 8.1 Pipeline Maintenance
- [ ] All opportunities reviewed for accuracy and stage validity
- [ ] Stale opportunities (>14 days no activity) addressed
- [ ] Forecast updated and submitted by deadline
- [ ] Pipeline metrics reviewed and anomalies investigated
- [ ] CRM data quality issues identified and resolved

### 8.2 Activity Execution
- [ ] Planned outreach activities completed as scheduled
- [ ] Customer meetings conducted and followed up
- [ ] Internal meetings attended and action items completed
- [ ] Training and development activities completed
- [ ] Administrative tasks (expenses, reporting) completed

### 8.3 Reporting and Communication
- [ ] Weekly sales report contributions completed by deadline
- [ ] Forecast submitted and validated
- [ ] Pipeline review meeting prepared for and attended
- [ ] Coaching and feedback sessions completed
- [ ] Knowledge sharing activities participated in

## 9. Quarterly Planning Definition of Done

Quarterly sales planning is considered "done" when:

### 9.1 Plan Development
- [ ] Quota attainment analysis completed for previous quarter
- [ ] Pipeline quality and trends analyzed
- [ ] Individual and team performance reviewed
- [ ] Market and competitive landscape assessed
- [ ] Sales quota and targets established for upcoming quarter
- [ ] Resource allocation and hiring needs determined

### 9.2 Plan Communication
- [ ] Quarterly plan documented and shared with sales team
- [ ] Individual quotas and goals communicated to representatives
- [ ] Resource plans and support commitments confirmed
- [ ] Training and development plans finalized

 plan reviewed and approved by CSO and CEO

## 10. Compliance Verification

To verify compliance with Definition of Standards:

### 10.1 Self-Assessment Checklist
Sales representatives should verify:
- [ ] Opportunity stage accurately reflects customer commitment level
- [ ] All required discovery information is recorded
- [ ] Qualification status is explicitly documented
- [ ] Next steps are clearly defined with owner and date
- [ ] CRM opportunity record is current and complete
- [ ] Forecast accurately reflects pipeline status
- [ ] Handoff materials are prepared when applicable

### 10.2 Manager Verification
Sales managers should verify during pipeline reviews:
- [ ] Opportunity stages are accurate and justifiable
- [ ] Required fields are complete in CRM records
- [ ] Qualification assessments are explicit and reasonable
- [ ] Next steps are specific, time-bound, and assigned
- [ ] Forecast submissions align with pipeline status
- [ ] Handoff readiness is assessed for late-stage opportunities

### 10.3 Audit Process
Periodic compliance audits will check:
- [ ] Random sample of opportunities for stage accuracy
- [ ] CRM record completeness for key information fields
- [ ] Forecast accuracy and submission timeliness
- [ ] Handoff package completeness for closed-won opportunities
- [ ] Adherence to defined sales process and stage gates
- [ ] Quality of qualification and discovery documentation
