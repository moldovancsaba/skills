## Every Employee — Start of Day

1. Review assigned priorities.
2. Review open blockers.
3. Review dependencies.
4. Confirm today's top 1-3 outcomes.
5. Verify location of source documents.
6. Update task status before beginning work.

## Every Employee — During the Day

1. Work only on prioritised or approved items.
2. Record decisions as they occur.
3. Save work only in approved locations.
4. Communicate blockers early.
5. Request clarification before continuing ambiguous work.
6. Follow the defined workflow and checklist.

## Every Employee — End of Day

1. Update status.
2. Record completed outputs.
3. Log remaining blockers.
4. Link documentation.
5. Prepare next-day priorities.
6. Ensure no official work remains trapped in personal locations.

## Managers — Daily

1. Confirm team priorities.
2. Verify active owners for all critical tasks.
3. Remove blockers where possible.
4. Review quality of documentation.
5. Verify handoffs are clear.
6. Challenge work marked done without evidence.
7. Escalate unresolved issues the same day.

## Executives — Daily

1. Review priority metrics.
2. Inspect major outputs.
3. Inspect cross-functional dependencies.
4. Review blockers and risks.
5. Ensure standards are followed inside the function.
6. Communicate material issues to the CEO.

## CEO — Daily

1. Review business-critical indicators.
2. Review executive updates.
3. Resolve cross-functional drift or conflict.
4. Ensure compliance is real, not nominal.
5. Remove executive-level blockers.
6. Maintain operating discipline across the company.
