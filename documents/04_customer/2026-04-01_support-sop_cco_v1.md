## Support Standard Operating Procedure

### 1. Issue Intake
- Receive issue from customer through approved channel
- Log issue immediately with timestamp, customer, and description
- Assign initial severity level (see Escalation Matrix)
- Acknowledge receipt to customer within 4 hours

### 2. Triage
- Classify issue type: technical, delivery, scope, billing, or other
- Assess severity and impact
- Identify required expertise or department
- Assign owner or route to appropriate team
- Set response deadline based on severity

### 3. Investigation
- Gather relevant context (customer history, previous communications, delivery records)
- Reproduce or verify the issue where applicable
- Identify root cause or likely cause
- Document findings in issue record

### 4. Resolution or Escalation
- If resolvable within scope: execute resolution, confirm with customer
- If outside scope or requires expertise: escalate per Escalation Matrix
- If requires product change: route to Product with customer signal
- If requires technical fix: route to Technology with issue details

### 5. Closure
- Confirm resolution with customer
- Log all actions taken and communications
- Capture feedback on support experience
- Close issue record with resolution summary
- Flag any systemic issues for review

## Escalation Matrix

| Severity | Definition | Response Time | Resolution Target | Escalation Path |
|----------|-----------|---------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **P1 — Critical** | Customer cannot use product, revenue at risk, or major trust issue | 1 hour | 4 hours | CCO → CEO immediately |
| **P2 — High** | Significant functionality impaired, customer blocked on key deliverable | 4 hours | 24 hours | CCO → CTO or CPO as needed |
| **P3 — Medium** | Partial impact, workaround available, customer inconvenienced | 24 hours | 72 hours | CCO internal handling |
| **P4 — Low** | Minor issue, cosmetic, or informational request | 48 hours | 5 business days | CCO internal handling |

### Escalation Triggers

**Escalate to CEO when:**
- Customer threatens churn or legal action
- Issue involves material revenue impact
- Cross-functional dispute cannot be resolved
- Scope dispute with commercial implications
- Reputational risk identified

**Escalate to CTO when:**
- Technical failure or system outage
- Deliverable quality issue requiring technical fix
- Infrastructure or access problem

**Escalate to CPO when:**
- Product feature gap causing customer issue
- Requirement misalignment discovered
- Customer request has product implications

**Escalate to CSO when:**
- Scope dispute (what was sold vs. what is expected)
- Commercial terms in question
- Upsell or renewal conversation triggered by issue

## Definition of Done

Support handling is not done unless:
- Issue is resolved or escalation path is documented
- Customer is informed of status and next steps
- All communications are logged
- Feedback is captured where relevant
- Product or technology implications are routed to the correct team
- Issue record is complete and closed or clearly marked as in-progress
