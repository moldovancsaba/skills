## 1. Department Mandate

Marketing owns market visibility, demand creation, positioning, messaging, and brand coherence. We turn market understanding into attention that the company can convert into pipeline and revenue.

## 2. Business Role

Marketing is the engine that generates qualified demand for {checklist}'s simple, actionable solutions. We ensure the right audience knows about us, understands our value, and engages with our brand.

## 3. Position in Company Value Chain

Marketing sits between **Market Insight** and **Go-to-Market Execution** in the company value chain:

1. Market insight → **Marketing (positioning, messaging, demand generation)** → Go-to-Market execution → Client delivery → Client usage and outcome → Feedback and iteration

## 4. Core Responsibilities

### 4.1 What We Own
- Market insight and audience understanding
- Positioning and category framing
- Messaging and brand coherence
- Campaign strategy and content strategy
- Channel strategy and demand generation
- Lead quality alignment with Sales
- Marketing reporting and asset governance
- Marketing workflow discipline

### 4.2 What We Do NOT Own
- Closing revenue (Sales)
- Product prioritisation (Product)
- Engineering delivery (Technology)
- Customer support execution (Customer Success)
- Pricing authority without Finance and CEO approval

## 5. Success Metrics

Marketing success is measured by:
- Qualified leads generated
- Marketing qualified leads (MQLs) accepted by Sales
- Campaign ROI
- Brand awareness metrics
- Content engagement metrics
- Channel performance metrics

## 6. Escalation Path

- **Normal:** CMO → CEO
- **Urgent:** Immediate escalation to CEO via approved communication channels
- **Cross-functional issues:** Escalate to CEO who convenes ExCom

## 7. Operating Standards

All marketing work follows company-wide operating rules. Marketing departmental standards are documented in the Marketing Operational Playbook section.

## 8. Required Deliverables

Marketing produces the following recurring deliverables:
- Weekly marketing performance report
- Campaign performance reports
- Content calendar
- Lead quality reports
- Brand compliance audits

## 9. Dependencies

Marketing depends on:
- **Product:** For product information, features, and roadmap insights
- **Sales:** For lead feedback, market signals, and conversion data
- **Technology:** For marketing technology stack support
- **Customer Success:** For customer insights and testimonials
- **CEO:** For strategic direction and approval of major campaigns

## 10. Review Cadence

- Weekly: Performance review with CEO
- Monthly: Strategic review with ExCom
- Quarterly: Planning and budget review
- Annually: Full marketing strategy review
