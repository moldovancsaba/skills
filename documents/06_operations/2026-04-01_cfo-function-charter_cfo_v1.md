## 1. Business Role

The CFO ensures the company's decisions are economically controlled, financially visible, and commercially disciplined.

The CFO prevents the company from lying to itself with bad numbers, vague pricing, and fantasy planning.

---

## 2. What the CFO Owns

- budgeting
- forecasting
- management reporting
- pricing guardrails
- revenue quality visibility
- cost visibility
- margin visibility
- approval thresholds
- financial controls
- commercial economics
- finance workflow discipline

## 3. What the CFO Does NOT Own

- campaign execution
- sales execution
- roadmap prioritisation
- engineering implementation
- customer support operations

---

## 4. Finance Value Chain

1. **Planning assumptions** — Define market, revenue, and cost assumptions based on strategy and input from ExCom.
2. **Budget setting** — Translate approved assumptions into departmental and company budgets.
3. **Spend control** — Monitor and enforce spending against approved budgets.
4. **Revenue and cost tracking** — Capture actuals against plan, identify gaps.
5. **Forecast updates** — Revise forward-looking estimates based on actuals and new information.
6. **Variance analysis** — Explain deviations, classify root causes, recommend corrective actions.
7. **Pricing and margin review** — Validate pricing against cost structure, margin targets, and market position.
8. **Executive reporting** — Deliver management reporting packs to CEO and ExCom.
9. **Corrective action recommendations** — Propose adjustments to spending, pricing, or planning assumptions.

## 5. Finance Workflows

### 5.1 Budgeting Workflow

| Stage | Owner | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| Planning assumptions drafted | CFO | Input from all ExCom members |
| Assumptions reviewed and approved | CEO | CEO sign-off |
| Departmental budgets created | CFO + department heads | Aligned to assumptions |
| Budget consolidated | CFO | No conflicts, totals verified |
| Budget approved | CEO | Formal approval |
| Budget published | CFO | All departments notified |

### 5.2 Forecasting Workflow

| Stage | Owner | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| Actuals collected | CFO | All departments submit |
| Variance calculated | CFO | Automated or manual reconciliation |
| Forecast revised | CFO | Assumptions updated where needed |
| Forecast reviewed | CEO | CEO sign-off on material changes |
| Forecast communicated | CFO | ExCom informed |

### 5.3 Management Reporting Workflow

| Stage | Owner | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| Data collection | CFO | All source data current |
| Report preparation | CFO | Numbers reconciled |
| Variance commentary | CFO | All material variances explained |
| Report reviewed | CEO | CEO sign-off |
| Report distributed | CFO | ExCom and stakeholders receive |

### 5.4 Pricing Approval Workflow

| Stage | Owner | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| Pricing proposal submitted | Requestor (CSO/CMO/CPO) | Within guardrail standard |
| Finance review | CFO | Margin and cost impact assessed |
| Approval or rejection | CFO (within threshold) or CEO (above threshold) | Recorded |
| Pricing updated | Requestor | System and communication updated |

### 5.5 Spend Approval Workflow

| Stage | Owner | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| Spend request submitted | Requestor | Within budget line |
| Finance review | CFO | Budget impact and approval threshold checked |
| Approval or rejection | Per approval matrix | Recorded |
| Spend executed | Requestor | Receipt/invoice captured |

---

## 8. Escalation Rules

- **Material variance (>10% or >threshold):** Escalate to CEO immediately.
- **Budget overrun risk:** Escalate to CEO with corrective options.
- **Pricing below margin floor:** Escalate to CEO before approval.
- **Revenue quality concern:** Escalate to CEO and CSO.
- **Unapproved spend detected:** Escalate to CEO and requesting department head.
