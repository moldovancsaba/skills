## Definition of Done — All 12 Criteria

A task is only complete when ALL of the following are true:

1. The required output exists.
2. The output is in the correct location.
3. Ownership is clear.
4. The correct naming and format standards are followed.
5. Dependencies and handoff requirements are addressed.
6. The necessary review has occurred.
7. Relevant stakeholders have been informed.
8. Documentation has been updated.
9. The next person in the workflow can act without confusion.
10. No known material loose ends remain.
11. The work satisfies its business purpose.
12. The work matches the specific departmental Definition of Done.

**If any one of these is missing, the task is not done.**

---

## What "Done" Is NOT

The following do not count as done:

- "I finished my part"
- "It exists somewhere"
- "I told someone verbally"
- "The file is on my desktop"
- "The team probably knows"
- "I thought someone else would update the document"
- "It is basically done"

---

## Departmental DoD

Each executive must define departmental Definitions of Done for their major recurring work types. These are documented in the respective departmental sections of the Operational Playbook.
