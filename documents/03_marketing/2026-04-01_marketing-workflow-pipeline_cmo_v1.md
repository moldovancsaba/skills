## 1. Marketing Funnel Stages

### Stage 1: Awareness
**Definition:** Prospect becomes aware of {checklist} and its solutions.
**Key Activities:** Content marketing, SEO, social media, PR, advertising
**Metrics:** Impressions, reach, website traffic, social followers
**Exit Criteria:** Prospect engages with content (click, visit, follow)
**Owner:** Content Team / Social Media Manager

### Stage 2: Interest
**Definition:** Prospect shows interest by consuming more content.
**Key Activities:** Blog posts, webinars, ebooks, case studies, newsletter
**Metrics:** Time on site, content downloads, email subscriptions
**Exit Criteria:** Prospect downloads gated content or subscribes to newsletter
**Owner:** Content Marketing / Demand Generation

### Stage 3: Consideration
**Definition:** Prospect evaluates {checklist} against alternatives.
**Key Activities:** Comparison guides, product demos, free trials, consultations
**Metrics:** Demo requests, trial signups, pricing page visits
**Exit Criteria:** Prospect requests a demo or starts a trial
**Owner:** Demand Generation / Sales Development

### Stage 4: Intent
**Definition:** Prospect shows buying intent.
**Key Activities:** Targeted campaigns, personalized outreach, retargeting
**Metrics:** Proposal requests, RFP submissions, purchase intent signals
**Exit Criteria:** Prospect enters sales pipeline
**Owner:** Sales Development / CMO

### Stage 5: Evaluation
**Definition:** Prospect is in active evaluation with Sales.
**Key Activities:** Sales enablement materials, case studies, ROI calculators
**Metrics:** Sales cycle length, win rate
**Exit Criteria:** Prospect becomes a customer
**Owner:** Sales Team / Marketing Support

## 3. Stage Gates

Each stage requires approval before proceeding to the next:

### Gate 1: Planning Approval
**Requirements:** Business case, audience definition, messaging alignment, budget approval
**Approver:** CMO / CEO (for major campaigns)

### Gate 2: Content Approval
**Requirements:** Brand compliance, message accuracy, legal review (if required)
**Approver:** CMO / Content Lead

### Gate 3: Launch Approval
**Requirements:** All assets ready, tracking set up, team briefed
**Approver:** CMO

### Gate 4: Lead Handoff Approval
**Requirements:** MQL criteria met, lead quality validated
**Approver:** CMO / Sales Lead

## 4. Workflow Triggers

| Trigger | Workflow Initiated |
|---------|-------------------|
| New product launch | Launch campaign workflow |
| Quarterly planning | Campaign planning workflow |
| Lead threshold not met | Lead generation workflow |
| Brand refresh | Brand update workflow |
| Market event | Event marketing workflow |

## 5. Workflow Documentation Requirements

All workflows must include:
- Clear start and end points
- Defined roles and responsibilities
- Approval gates
- Success criteria
- Escalation paths
- Templates and tools
- Performance metrics

## 6. Workflow Compliance

- All recurring marketing work must follow documented workflows
- Deviations require CMO approval
- Non-compliance escalates to CEO
- Weekly workflow compliance audits
