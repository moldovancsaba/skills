## Naming Standard

All product documents follow: `YYYY-MM-DD_topic_owner_version.md`

Example: `2026-04-01_requirements-standard_cpo_v1.md`

## Version Control

- Version number increments on substantive change
- Superseded documents moved to /archive/
- Current version always in main directory

## Access and Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| CPO | Full |
| Product Team | Read/Write |
| Engineering | Read |
| Other teams | As needed |

## Maintenance

Quarterly audit of product documentation:
- Verify all documents in correct locations
- Check naming compliance
- Archive outdated versions
- Update review dates
