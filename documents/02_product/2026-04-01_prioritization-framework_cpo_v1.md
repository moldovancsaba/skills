## Prioritisation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Customer Impact | 30% | How many customers benefit and by how much |
| Business Value | 25% | Revenue impact, cost savings, strategic alignment |
| Effort | 20% | Engineering and resource complexity |
| Time to Value | 15% | Speed of delivering customer benefit |
| Risk | 10% | Implementation and market risk |

## Scoring Process

1. Request received via Feedback Intake Process
2. Requirements documented
3. Each criterion scored 1-5
4. Weighted score calculated
5. Ranked against backlog
6. Top items added to roadmap

## Decision Authority

| Score Range | Decision |
|-------------|----------|
| 80-100 | Immediate priority |
| 60-79 | Near-term priority |
| 40-59 | Backlog |
| 0-39 | Deprioritised |

## Review Cycle

Roadmap reviewed every 2 weeks at Product Review meeting.
