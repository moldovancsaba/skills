## 1. Pipeline Management Rules

### 1.1 Pipeline Hygiene Standards
All sales representatives must maintain pipeline hygiene according to these standards:

- **Stage Accuracy:** Opportunities must reflect the correct sales stage based on completed activities and customer commitments
- **Data Completeness:** All required fields in CRM must be populated for each opportunity
- **Activity Logging:** All customer interactions must be logged within 24 hours
- **Forecast Updates:** Pipeline forecasts must be updated weekly or when significant changes occur
- **Stale Opportunity Review:** Opportunities with no activity for >14 days must be reviewed and either advanced or disqualified

### 1.2 Pipeline Metrics and Health Indicators
Key pipeline health metrics that must be maintained:

- **Pipeline Velocity:** Average time opportunities spend in each stage
- **Stage Conversion Rates:** Percentage of opportunities moving from one stage to next
- **Pipeline Coverage Ratio:** Minimum 3x coverage of quarterly quota
- **Weighted Pipeline Value:** Sum of opportunity value × probability weight by stage
- **Forecast Accuracy:** Variance between forecasted and actual booked revenue

### 1.3 Opportunity Management Rules
Specific rules governing opportunity creation and management:

- **Minimum Deal Size:** Opportunities must exceed $5,000 ACV to be entered into pipeline
- **Required Stakeholder Engagement:** Minimum 2 stakeholder interactions before Stage 3
- **Solution Validation:** Technical validation completed before proposal (Stage 4)
- **Competitive Awareness:** Competitive landscape documented for all Stage 3+ opportunities
- **Decision Process Mapping:** Formal decision criteria and process documented by Stage 4

## 2. Proposal Workflow Standard

### 2.1 Proposal Initiation Criteria
Proposals may only be initiated when:

- Discovery phase completed with documented business needs
- Solution fit validated through technical discussion
- Preliminary budget alignment confirmed
- Access to economic buyer or champion established
- Internal solution review completed and approved

### 2.2 Proposal Development Process
Standardized steps for proposal creation:

#### 2.2.1 Solution Configuration
- Select appropriate check solution components based on client needs
- Validate technical feasibility and implementation requirements
- Configure pricing according to volume and term considerations
- Document assumptions and dependencies

#### 2.2.2 Proposal Content Requirements
All proposals must include:

- Executive Summary: Business problem and proposed solution
- Solution Overview: Detailed description of recommended approach
- Implementation Plan: Timeline, milestones, and resource requirements
- Investment Summary: Clear pricing breakdown and payment terms
- Value Proposition: Quantified business benefits and ROI
- Terms & Conditions: Standard check agreement terms
- Acceptance Procedure: Clear next steps for client approval

#### 2.2.3 Internal Review Process
Before client presentation, proposals must undergo:

- Solution Review: Validation by sales engineer or technical lead
- Pricing Review: Verification against pricing guidelines and approval thresholds
- Legal Review: Standard terms confirmation (for non-standard terms)
- Manager Approval: Final sign-off by sales manager

### 2.3 Proposal Delivery and Validation
Standard process for proposal presentation and client validation:

#### 2.3.1 Presentation Standards
- Formal presentation to all identified stakeholders
- Clear agenda distributed in advance
- Solution demonstration included (when applicable)
- Time for Q&A and discussion allocated
- Presentation materials delivered to client within 24 hours

#### 2.3.2 Feedback Incorporation
Process for handling client feedback:

- Feedback captured and categorized (clarification, concern, modification request)
- Internal review of requested changes for feasibility
- Revised proposal delivered within agreed timeframe
- Documentation of all changes and rationale

#### 2.3.3 Validation Checklist
Before moving to negotiation stage, confirm:

- All stakeholder concerns addressed
- Solution aligns with documented business requirements
- Pricing acceptable and within budget expectations
- Implementation timeline acceptable to client
- Legal terms reviewed and acceptable
- Clear path to agreement established

## 3. Pipeline Review Cadence

### 3.1 Weekly Pipeline Review
Mandatory weekly review with CSO:

- Review of all Stage 3+ opportunities
- Forecast updates and adjustments
- Identification of pipeline risks and mitigation plans
- Resource allocation decisions
- Coaching and skill development focus areas

### 3.2 Monthly Pipeline Deep Dive
Monthly review with ExCom:

- Pipeline health metrics analysis
- Trend identification and forecasting refinement
- Strategic pipeline adjustments
- Competitive landscape assessment
- Resource planning for upcoming periods

## 4. Pipeline Risk Management

### 4.1 Risk Identification Framework
Standard categories for pipeline risks:

- **Timeline Risk:** Potential delays in decision or implementation
- **Budget Risk:** Funding uncertainty or constraints
- **Competitive Risk:** Active competitive evaluation or pressure
- **Stakeholder Risk:** Key stakeholder disengagement or turnover
- **Technical Risk:** Implementation complexity or integration concerns
- **Process Risk:** Client procurement or approval process delays

### 4.2 Risk Mitigation Standards
Required actions for identified risks:

- **High Risk:** Mitigation plan developed within 48 hours, escalated to CSO
- **Medium Risk:** Mitigation plan developed within 5 business days
- **Low Risk:** Monitored and addressed in regular pipeline reviews

All risks must be documented in CRM with:
- Risk description and potential impact
- Probability assessment (High/Medium/Low)
- Mitigation plan and owner
- Target resolution date
- Status updates tracked weekly
