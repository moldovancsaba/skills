## 1. Qualification Criteria Framework

{checklist} uses the BANT qualification framework enhanced with solution-specific criteria:

### 1.1 BANT Criteria
- **Budget:** Confirmed budget allocation or ability to secure funds for solution
- **Authority:** Access to decision-maker or champion who can influence purchase
- **Need:** Documented business problem that our solution addresses
- **Timeline:** Defined timeline for evaluation and purchase decision

### 1.2 Solution-Specific Qualification
- **Solution Fit:** Clear match between client needs and {checklist} solution capabilities
- **Technical Feasibility:** Solution can be implemented in client's technical environment
- **Organizational Readiness:** Client has capacity to adopt and utilize solution effectively
- **Value Proposition Alignment:** Client recognizes and values our unique proposition

## 2. Sales Stage Definitions

{checklist} sales process consists of six standardized stages:

### 2.1 Stage 1: Lead Qualification
**Purpose:** Initial assessment of lead quality and fit
**Entry Criteria:** Lead generated from marketing or outbound prospecting
**Exit Criteria:** 
- BANT criteria minimally met (at least 3/4)
- Initial solution fit identified
- Contact established with appropriate stakeholder
**Required Activities:**
- Initial outreach and discovery call
- Basic qualification assessment
- Lead enrichment and research
**Duration Target:** ≤ 3 business days

### 2.2 Stage 2: Discovery & Needs Analysis
**Purpose:** Deep dive into client challenges and requirements
**Entry Criteria:** Lead qualified as sales-ready opportunity
**Exit Criteria:**
- Comprehensive understanding of client business problem
- Documented current state and desired future state
- Identified key stakeholders and decision process
- Preliminary ROI estimation completed
**Required Activities:**
- Detailed discovery sessions (minimum 2)
- Stakeholder mapping
- Current state analysis
- Solution positioning and value discussion
**Duration Target:** ≤ 7 business days

### 2.3 Stage 3: Solution Design
**Purpose:** Develop tailored solution proposal
**Entry Criteria:** Discovery completed with confirmed need and fit
**Exit Criteria:**
- Solution architecture defined
- Implementation approach outlined
- Resource requirements estimated
- Preliminary pricing developed
**Required Activities:**
- Solution design workshop
- Technical validation (if needed)
- Pricing configuration
- Internal solution review
**Duration Target:** ≤ 10 business days

### 2.4 Stage 4: Proposal & Validation
**Purpose:** Formal proposal presentation and validation
**Entry Criteria:** Solution design completed and approved internally
**Exit Criteria:**
- Formal proposal delivered and reviewed
- Client feedback incorporated
- Legal/compliance review initiated (if required)
- Decision criteria confirmed with client
**Required Activities:**
- Proposal creation and review
- Formal presentation to stakeholders
- Q&A and objection handling
- Next steps agreement
**Duration Target:** ≤ 5 business days

### 2.5 Stage 5: Negotiation & Commitment
**Purpose:** Finalize terms and secure commitment
**Entry Criteria:** Proposal validated and client expresses intent to proceed
**Exit Criteria:**
- Contract terms negotiated and agreed
- Legal review completed
- Purchase order or contract signed
- Implementation kickoff scheduled
**Required Activities:**
- Contract negotiation
- Legal and compliance review
- Pricing finalization
- Executive sponsorship confirmation
**Duration Target:** ≤ 8 business days

### 2.6 Stage 6: Closed Won/Lost
**Purpose:** Finalize outcome and transition to delivery or capture learning
**Entry Criteria:** Negotiation completed or decision made not to proceed
**Exit Criteria (Won):**
- Contract executed and deposited
- Handoff package completed and delivered to CCO
- Forecast updated to committed
**Exit Criteria (Lost):**
- Loss reason documented and categorized
- Competitive intelligence captured
- Relationship preservation actions defined
**Required Activities:**
- Contract execution (if won)
- Handoff preparation (if won)
- Loss analysis (if lost)
- Forecast update
**Duration Target:** ≤ 2 business days for completion activities

## 3. Stage Gate Requirements

Each stage transition requires:
- Completion of all required activities
- Documentation of key findings in CRM
- Manager validation and approval
- Clear next steps defined with owner and date
- Updated probability weighting

## 4. Probability Weighting by Stage

- Stage 1 (Lead Qualification): 10%
- Stage 2 (Discovery & Needs Analysis): 25%
- Stage 3 (Solution Design): 40%
- Stage 4 (Proposal & Validation): 60%
- Stage 5 (Negotiation & Commitment): 80%
- Stage 6 (Closed Won): 100%
- Stage 6 (Closed Lost): 0%

## 5. Disqualification Criteria

Opportunities should be disqualified if:
- Budget not available and unlikely to be secured
- No access to economic buyer or decision influencer
- No demonstrable business need or fit
- Timeline exceeds solution relevance window (>6 months)
- Client unwilling to provide necessary information for qualification
- Legal or compliance barriers identified
- Solution requires capabilities outside our roadmap without exceptions
