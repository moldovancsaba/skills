## Communication Principles

All client communication must be:
- **Clear** — No ambiguity about what is being communicated
- **Concise** — No unnecessary content, get to the point
- **Traceable** — Every communication is logged in the customer record
- **Timely** — Sent when promised or earlier, never late without notice
- **Professional** — Consistent tone, correct formatting, error-free

## Communication Cadence

| Touchpoint | Frequency | Owner | Channel | Purpose |
|------------|----------|-------|---------|---------|
| Onboarding kickoff | Once per customer | CCO | Scheduled call or async | Set expectations, introduce process |
| Delivery notification | Per delivery | CCO | Written | Notify of deliverable, include instructions |
| Status update | Weekly or per milestone | CCO | Written | Current status, next steps, blockers |
| Check-in | Monthly | CCO | Call or written | Relationship health, feedback, adjustments |
| Issue response | Per issue severity | CCO | Written or call | Acknowledge, update, resolve |
| Renewal approach | 60 days before renewal | CCO | Written + call | Prepare for renewal, surface signals |

## Communication Types

### Welcome Communication
**When:** After contract receipt, before kickoff.
**Content:** Welcome message, assigned owner and contact, kickoff details, what to expect, communication preferences confirmation.

### Kickoff Summary
**When:** Within 24 hours of kickoff meeting.
**Content:** Meeting summary, confirmed scope/deliverables/timeline, stakeholder roles, communication cadence, next steps with owners/deadlines, open items.

### Progress Update
**When:** Per agreed cadence or at each milestone.
**Content:** Current status, completed since last update, in progress, next, risks/blockers/decisions needed, next update date.

### Delivery Notification
**When:** When a deliverable is handed to the client.
**Content:** What is being delivered, how to access, required client actions, deadline for feedback, support contact.

### Issue or Escalation Communication
**When:** When an issue arises or is escalated.
**Content:** Issue description, impact on client, actions being taken, expected resolution timeline, escalation path, next update time.

### Resolution Confirmation
**When:** When an issue is resolved.
**Content:** What was resolved, how, preventative measures, confirmation of satisfaction request, feedback request.

### Check-In or Review Communication
**When:** Per agreed review cadence or at engagement milestones.
**Content:** Engagement summary, deliverables completed, satisfaction check, concerns or adjustments, forward plan, feedback request.

## Message Structure

Every client communication must include:
1. **Subject/Title** — Clear description of the communication purpose
2. **Context** — Why this communication is being sent
3. **Content** — The actual information, deliverable, or request
4. **Action Required** — What the customer needs to do (if anything), with deadline
5. **Next Steps** — What happens next and when
6. **Contact** — Who to reach out to with questions

## Communication Channels

| Channel | Use Case | Response Expectation |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| Email | Formal communication, deliverables, summaries | Per SLA |
| Scheduled meeting | Kickoff, reviews, complex discussions | As scheduled |
| Agreed messaging | Quick questions, updates | Per SLA |

All meaningful communication must be logged regardless of channel.

## Communication Logging

Every client communication must be logged with:
- Date and time
- Channel used
- Participants
- Summary of content
- Any decisions made
- Any actions required with owner and deadline
- Link to relevant deliverable or record

## Communication Rules

- No verbal-only commitments to clients — all commitments must be written
- If a verbal discussion occurs, a written summary must follow within 24 hours
- The client must never be left without a clear status update
- Bad news must be communicated early, directly, and with a proposed path forward
- Communication must always name the next owner and next action
- All communication must be stored in the client account record
- Never communicate scope changes without CSO and CEO awareness
- Never communicate product roadmap or technical decisions without CPO/CTO alignment
