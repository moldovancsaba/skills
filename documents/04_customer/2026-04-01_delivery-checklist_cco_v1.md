# Delivery Checklist

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure every client delivery meets quality standards and follows the approved workflow.
**Cadence:** Per delivery
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Confirm deliverable specifications against contract or scope document
- [ ] Identify and allocate internal resources required
- [ ] Create delivery timeline with milestones
- [ ] Share delivery plan with client for confirmation
- [ ] Receive client confirmation of delivery plan
- [ ] Execute deliverable per specifications
- [ ] Provide progress updates per communication cadence
- [ ] Flag any scope changes, risks, or blockers immediately
- [ ] Perform internal quality check against specifications
- [ ] Deliver output to client via agreed channel
- [ ] Notify client of delivery with summary and required actions
- [ ] Request and confirm client receipt
- [ ] Request client review and feedback within agreed timeframe
- [ ] Address any revision requests per scope terms
- [ ] Obtain written client acceptance
- [ ] Capture feedback on delivery experience
- [ ] Route product or technology implications to relevant departments
- [ ] Open support window per agreement terms
- [ ] Update account status to active/support
- [ ] Schedule next touchpoint or review if applicable
- [ ] Archive delivery record with all communications and outputs

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked, the client has accepted the deliverable in writing, feedback is captured, and the delivery record is archived.

### Definition of Done

- The client received the required output
- Communication is logged
- The current status is clear
- The next owner is clear
- Unresolved issues are named
- Escalation path is documented where needed
- Feedback is captured
- Product implications are routed to Product
- Delivery or quality implications are routed to Technology
- Delivery record is archived in the correct location

### Storage

All delivery outputs are stored in the client's account record under `/Users/Shared/Projects/checklist/documents/04_customer/` with client-specific subdirectories as needed.
