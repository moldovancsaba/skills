## Work Type: Feature Requirement

**Definition of Done:**
- Problem clearly defined
- Target user identified
- Expected outcome stated
- User story written
- Acceptance criteria are testable
- Dependencies identified
- Technology feasibility reviewed
- Customer impact assessed
- Marketing impact assessed

---

## Work Type: Product Decision

**Definition of Done:**
- Context documented
- Options considered
- Rationale recorded
- Stakeholder input captured
- Decision logged
- Follow-up actions assigned
- Relevant parties informed

---

## Work Type: Release

**Definition of Done:**
- All acceptance criteria met
- No known critical bugs
- Documentation updated
- Sign-offs obtained
- Customer communication prepared
- Marketing launch ready
- Rollback plan in place
- Team debrief completed

---

## Work Type: Roadmap Update

**Definition of Done:**
- Feedback incorporated
- Priorities re-evaluated
- Dependencies checked
- Stakeholders consulted
- Document updated
- Changes communicated

---

## Work Type: Customer Feedback Response

**Definition of Done:**
- Feedback logged
- Categorised correctly
- Triaged within SLA
- Customer response sent
- Added to backlog if applicable
