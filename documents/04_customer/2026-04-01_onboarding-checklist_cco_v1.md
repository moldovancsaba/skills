# Onboarding Checklist

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure every new client is onboarded consistently and completely.
**Cadence:** Per new client
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Verify signed contract is received and complete
- [ ] Confirm scope, deliverables, timeline, and pricing match contract
- [ ] Create client account record with all contact details
- [ ] Assign primary CCO owner to the account
- [ ] Schedule kickoff meeting within 3 business days
- [ ] Send welcome email with kickoff details and agenda
- [ ] Conduct kickoff meeting
- [ ] Confirm client stakeholders and their roles
- [ ] Confirm scope, deliverables, and timeline with client
- [ ] Confirm communication preferences and cadence
- [ ] Confirm success criteria with client
- [ ] Document and share kickoff summary within 24 hours
- [ ] Configure client workspace or access as required
- [ ] Set up internal tracking for the account
- [ ] Create delivery schedule with milestones
- [ ] Share access credentials or onboarding materials with client
- [ ] Confirm client can access required tools or materials
- [ ] Get client confirmation that onboarding is complete

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked, the client has confirmed onboarding is complete, and the account status is set to active.

### Definition of Done

- Client has received and acknowledged welcome communication
- Kickoff meeting is completed and summary is shared
- Client has access to all required tools and materials
- Delivery schedule is confirmed by client
- Account is active in internal tracking
- Next owner and next action are documented

### Storage

All onboarding outputs are stored in the client's account record under `/Users/Shared/Projects/checklist/documents/04_customer/` with client-specific subdirectories as needed.
