## Required Workflow Stages

Every work item follows the same baseline lifecycle:

1. **Requested** — A request exists. States what is wanted and why it matters.
2. **Clarified** — Scope, expected output, owner, dependency, and timing are clear.
3. **Approved** — A manager or executive confirms the work should proceed.
4. **Planned** — Work is prioritised, sequenced, and placed into the proper queue.
5. **In Progress** — The owner is actively executing.
6. **Reviewed** — The relevant reviewer checks against standards and Definition of Done.
7. **Done** — The work has met all completion requirements.
8. **Archived / Measured** — Output is stored properly and any result measurement is captured.

## No Skipping Rule

No work may skip from Requested directly to Done. No work may skip review unless explicitly exempted by a manager or executive.

## Handoff Standard

Every handoff must include:
- What is being handed off
- Expected output or action
- Deadline
- Owner of next step
- Location of relevant documents
