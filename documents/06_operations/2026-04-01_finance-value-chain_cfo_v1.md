## Finance Value Chain Stages

### 1. Budgeting & Forecasting
**Purpose:** Establish financial targets and predict future performance to guide resource allocation.
**Inputs:** 
- Historical financial data
- Strategic objectives from CEO/Strategy team
- Sales pipeline and revenue forecasts from CCO/CMO
- Headcount plans from People team
- Technology investment plans from CTO
**Outputs:**
- Annual budget
- Quarterly forecasts updated monthly
- Departmental spending limits
- Cash flow projections
**Gate to Next Stage:** Budget approval by Executive Committee

### 2. Financial Transaction Processing
**Purpose:** Accurately record and classify all financial transactions in compliance with accounting standards.
**Inputs:**
- Source documents (invoices, receipts, contracts, expense reports)
- Payroll data
- Bank statements
- Revenue recognition triggers
**Outputs:**
- General ledger entries
- Trial balance
- Financial statements preparation inputs
**Gate to Next Stage:** Monthly close completion and reconciliation sign-off

### 3. Management Reporting & Analysis
**Purpose:** Transform financial data into actionable insights for decision-makers.
**Inputs:**
- Closed financial data
- Operational metrics from other departments
- Variance analysis requirements
**Outputs:**
- Monthly management reporting pack
- Variance analysis reports
- KPI dashboards
- Ad-hoc financial analysis
**Gate to Next Stage:** Management review and question resolution

### 4. Pricing & Commercial Economics
**Purpose:** Ensure pricing strategies are financially sound and commercially viable.
**Inputs:**
- Cost data from operations
- Market pricing intelligence from Marketing/Sales
- Customer value perception data
- Competitive landscape analysis
**Outputs:**
- Pricing guardrails and approval matrix
- Contribution margin analysis by product/service
- Customer profitability analysis
- Discount authorization framework
**Gate to Next Stage:** Pricing committee approval of new structures

### 5. Financial Controls & Compliance
**Purpose:** Safeguard assets, ensure data integrity, and maintain regulatory compliance.
**Inputs:**
- Financial transactions and balances
- Control framework requirements (SOX, GAAP, etc.)
- Risk assessments
**Outputs:**
- Control effectiveness reports
- Exception and breach reports
- Audit work papers
- Compliance certifications
**Gate to Next Stage:** Control deficiency remediation verification

### 6. Treasury & Cash Management
**Purpose:** Optimize liquidity, manage financial risk, and support capital structure decisions.
**Inputs:**
- Cash flow forecasts
- Working capital requirements
- Investment opportunities
- Debt covenant requirements
**Outputs:**
- Cash positioning reports
- Investment recommendations
- Debt management plans
- Foreign exchange risk mitigation
**Gate to Next Stage:** Treasury policy compliance verification

### 7. Financial Close & Reporting
**Purpose:** Produce accurate, timely financial statements for internal and external stakeholders.
**Inputs:**
- All processed financial transactions
- Adjustments and provisions
- Disclosure requirements
**Outputs:**
- Internal financial statements
- Management reporting package
- External reporting inputs (if applicable)
- Variance explanations
**Gate to Next Stage:** Executive review and sign-off

## Cross-Functional Linkages

### To Strategy Team (01_strategy/)
- Provides financial modeling for strategic initiatives
- Receives strategic priorities for budget alignment
- Shares financial performance trends for strategy refinement

### To Product Team (02_product/)
- Provides unit economics analysis for product decisions
- Receives product roadmap for investment planning
- Shares pricing impact analysis on product profitability

### To Marketing Team (03_marketing/)
- Sets customer acquisition cost (CAC) targets
- Receives campaign ROI data for effectiveness analysis
- Shares LTV:CAC ratios for channel optimization

### To Customer Team (04_customer/)
- Defines pricing and discount structures for customer agreements
- Receives renewal and churn data for revenue forecasting
- Shares customer profitability insights for account strategy

### To Technology Team (05_technology/)
- Approves technology investments based on ROI analysis
- Receives technology expense data for budget tracking
- Shares cost optimization opportunities from vendor contracts

### To People Team (07_people/)
- Manages payroll and benefits accounting
- Receives headcount plans for budget forecasting
- Shares compensation cost analysis for planning

## Key Finance Deliverables by Frequency

### Daily
- Cash position monitoring
- Expense submission processing
- Revenue collection tracking

### Weekly
- Cash flow forecasting update
- Variance analysis preparation
- Accounts receivable/payable aging review
- Compliance exception monitoring

### Monthly
- Management reporting pack
- Budget vs. actual variance review
- Forecast updates
- KPI dashboard refresh
- Control effectiveness testing
- Pricing exception reporting

### Quarterly
- Rolling forecast extension
- Strategic initiative financial review
- Pricing structure review
- Capital allocation assessment
- External reporting preparation (if applicable)

### Annually
- Budget development process
- Long-term financial planning
- External audit coordination
- Tax planning and compliance
- Compensation and benefits review
