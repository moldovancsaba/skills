## 1. Onboarding Workflow

### Stage 1: Contract Received

**Trigger:** Signed contract or agreement received from Sales/CEO.

- [ ] Confirm contract details are complete (scope, deliverables, timeline, pricing, contacts)
- [ ] Create client account record
- [ ] Assign primary CCO owner for the account
- [ ] Schedule kickoff meeting within 3 business days
- [ ] Send welcome communication to client with kickoff details

**Gate:** Contract details verified, kickoff scheduled, client notified.

### Stage 2: Kickoff

**Trigger:** Kickoff meeting with client.

- [ ] Confirm client stakeholder contacts and roles
- [ ] Confirm scope, deliverables, and timeline
- [ ] Confirm communication preferences and cadence
- [ ] Confirm success criteria with client
- [ ] Identify any client-side dependencies or blockers
- [ ] Document kickoff summary and share with client within 24 hours

**Gate:** Kickoff summary sent, client confirms understanding, delivery plan agreed.

### Stage 3: Account Setup

**Trigger:** Kickoff complete.

- [ ] Configure client workspace or access as required
- [ ] Set up internal tracking for the account
- [ ] Create delivery schedule with milestones
- [ ] Share access credentials or onboarding materials with client
- [ ] Confirm client can access required tools or materials

**Gate:** Client has access, delivery schedule confirmed, account is active.

## 2. Delivery Workflow

### Stage 1: Delivery Planning

**Trigger:** Account setup complete, delivery is scheduled.

- [ ] Confirm deliverable specifications against contract
- [ ] Identify internal resources and dependencies required
- [ ] Create delivery timeline with milestones
- [ ] Share delivery plan with client for confirmation
- [ ] Confirm client acceptance of delivery plan

**Gate:** Delivery plan confirmed by client, internal resources allocated.

### Stage 2: Execution

**Trigger:** Delivery plan approved.

- [ ] Execute deliverable per specifications
- [ ] Provide progress updates per communication cadence
- [ ] Flag any scope changes, risks, or blockers immediately
- [ ] Route product or technology implications to relevant departments
- [ ] Maintain delivery log with dates, actions, and decisions

**Gate:** Deliverable complete, internally reviewed against quality standards.

### Stage 3: Delivery Handoff

**Trigger:** Deliverable ready for client review.

- [ ] Perform internal quality check against agreed specifications
- [ ] Deliver output to client via agreed channel
- [ ] Notify client of delivery with summary and any required actions
- [ ] Request client confirmation of receipt
- [ ] Log delivery in client account record

**Gate:** Deliverable delivered, client notified, receipt confirmed.

### Stage 4: Client Acceptance

**Trigger:** Client has received the deliverable.

- [ ] Request client review and feedback within agreed timeframe
- [ ] Address any revision requests per scope terms
- [ ] Confirm client acceptance in writing
- [ ] Capture feedback for improvement
- [ ] Route any product or technology implications to relevant departments

**Gate:** Client acceptance confirmed, feedback captured, delivery closed.

### Stage 5: Post-Delivery

**Trigger:** Client acceptance confirmed.

- [ ] Open support window per agreement terms
- [ ] Request feedback on delivery experience
- [ ] Update account status to active/support
- [ ] Schedule next touchpoint or review if applicable
- [ ] Archive delivery record with all communications and outputs

**Gate:** Support window open, feedback captured, account status updated.
