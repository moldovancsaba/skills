# Communication Template

Use this pattern for all meaningful work updates.

---

## Update Pattern

1. **Situation** — What is the current state?
2. **Problem** — What is wrong, missing, or at risk?
3. **Decision** — What has been decided?
4. **Owner** — Who is responsible for the next action?
5. **Deadline** — When must it be done?
6. **Risk if missed** — What happens if the deadline is not met?
7. **Next review point** — When will this be checked again?

---

## Comment Template

```markdown
## Status

[One-line status summary]

## What Changed

- [Change 1]
- [Change 2]

## What Is Needed

- [Action needed from whom]

## Owner

[Named person for next action]

## Deadline

[Date or cadence]

## Next Review

[When this will be checked]
```
