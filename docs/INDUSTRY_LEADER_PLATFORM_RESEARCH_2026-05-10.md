## Distilled Market Truth

Across these platforms, the strongest repeated patterns are:

1. search and answers must be grounded in connected company context
2. AI must move from answer to action inside the same workflow
3. operators need governance, observability, and usage controls
4. reusable workflow components beat one-off prompt tricks
5. deep research and enrichment need citation durability and conflict handling
6. human override must be simple, visible, and reversible

## Recommended Product Principle

For CHECKLIST, the winning angle is not to copy every leader.

The better position is:

- card-based autonomous intelligence
- durable provenance and explainability
- simple HiTL control for humans
- agent/workflow orchestration that stays embedded in real operating work

That means we should favor:

- fewer, more composable primitives
- stronger evidence and observability
- direct movement from research -> knowledge -> planning -> checklist -> delivery
