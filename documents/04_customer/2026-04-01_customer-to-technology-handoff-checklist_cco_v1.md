# Checklist: Customer-to-Technology Issue Handoff

**Owner:** CCO
**Purpose:** Ensure customer technical issues are routed to CTO with full context
**Cadence:** Per technical issue requiring Technology
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-01
**Status:** Active

---

### Steps

- [ ] Capture issue details: description, impact, customer context, severity
- [ ] Gather relevant technical context (error messages, delivery records, access logs)
- [ ] Assess urgency against Escalation Matrix
- [ ] Prepare handoff package: issue details, impact, customer details, severity, steps to reproduce
- [ ] Route to CTO with clear priority
- [ ] Log handoff in customer record
- [ ] Inform customer of escalation and expected timeline
- [ ] Track CTO acknowledgment and progress
- [ ] Relay resolution to customer when received
- [ ] Confirm resolution with customer
- [ ] Close issue record

### Completion Criteria

This checklist is complete when all steps are checked and the Definition of Done is satisfied.

### Definition of Done

- Issue is captured with full technical context
- Issue is routed to CTO with clear priority
- Customer is informed of escalation and timeline
- CTO acknowledgment and progress are tracked
- Resolution is relayed to customer
- Customer confirms resolution
- Issue record is closed

### Storage

Completed checklists stored in customer account record under 04_customer/
