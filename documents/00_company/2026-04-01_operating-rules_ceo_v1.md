## Rule 1 — One company, one operating method

No function, department, or individual may operate with self-invented standards when a company standard exists.

## Rule 2 — Official work belongs in official locations

All official work must be stored in approved directories under /Users/moldovancsaba/Projects/checklist/documents.

## Rule 3 — No undocumented decisions, handoffs, or process changes

Any material decision, handoff, requirement, process, approval, or deliverable must be documented.

## Rule 4 — No recurring work without a checklist or SOP

If it repeats, it becomes a checklist, SOP, or documented workflow.

## Rule 5 — No task without business purpose

Every meaningful task must be linked to business value.

## Rule 6 — No work marked done without review against Definition of Done

Completion means verified completion, not personal belief.

## Rule 7 — No vague ownership

Every task, document, process, and recurring workflow must have one named owner.

## Rule 8 — No uncontrolled local systems

Personal folders, personal naming schemes, and ad hoc tracking methods may be used only as temporary working notes, never as official operating records.

## Rule 9 — No invisible decisions

Verbal decisions must be written down after agreement.

## Rule 10 — No bypassing the chain without cause

Normal escalation and communication must follow the approved structure.

---

## Enforcement

The CEO enforces these rules through the Executive Committee. Weekly compliance audits. Non-compliance is corrected immediately.
