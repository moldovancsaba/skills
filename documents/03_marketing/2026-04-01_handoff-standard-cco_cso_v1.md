## 1. Handoff Trigger Criteria

The handoff from Sales to Customer Success (CCO) is triggered when:

- Contract is fully executed and deposited (if applicable)
- Opportunity reaches Stage 6: Closed Won in CRM
- Implementation kickoff date is scheduled within 30 days
- All pre-implementation requirements are documented and confirmed
- Sales has completed all required discovery and validation activities

## 2. Handoff Package Requirements

A complete handoff package must include the following elements:

### 2.1 Contractual Documentation
- Fully executed contract or purchase order
- Statement of Work (SOW) if applicable
- Order form with detailed solution configuration
- Payment terms and schedule
- Any amendments or addendums

### 2.2 Solution Configuration Details
- Specific check solution components purchased
- Customization requirements (if any)
- Integration points with client systems
- Technical specifications and requirements
- Implementation approach and methodology

### 2.3 Client Information
- Primary contact information (name, title, email, phone)
- Technical contact information
- Executive sponsor information
- Billing contact information
- Implementation team contacts
- Organizational structure and decision-making process

### 2.4 Business Context
- Documented business problem and objectives
- Success criteria and expected outcomes
- ROI expectations and value realization timeline
- Key performance indicators (KPIs) to track
- Competitive situation and why client chose check

### 2.5 Sales Process Artifacts
- Discovery notes and requirements documentation
- Solution design documents and architecture diagrams
- Proposal and presentation materials
- Email correspondence and meeting summaries
- Objection handling records and resolutions
- Competitive comparison documentation (if relevant)

### 2.6 Implementation Requirements
- Required client resources and time commitments
- Technical prerequisites and dependencies
- Training requirements and participant identification
- Data migration requirements (if applicable)
- Change management considerations
- Go-live criteria and acceptance testing plan

### 2.7 Timeline and Milestones
- Implementation kickoff date
- Key milestone dates and deliverables
- Target go-live date
- Post-implementation review schedule
- Renewal or expansion discussion timeline

## 3. Handoff Process Steps

### 3.1 Pre-Handoff Preparation (Sales Responsibility)
Upon contract execution:
1. Notify CCO of won opportunity via automated CRM notification
2. Schedule handoff meeting within 2 business days
3. Gather and organize all handoff package elements
4. Complete internal handoff checklist
5. Prepare solution overview presentation for handoff meeting

### 3.2 Handoff Meeting (Joint Responsibility)
Mandatory meeting between Sales and Customer Success:
1. **Sales Presentation:** Solution overview, business context, and client expectations
2. **Solution Review:** Technical walkthrough of configured solution
3. **Client Introduction:** Transfer of relationships and communication protocols
4. **Expectation Alignment:** Confirmation of timelines, milestones, and success criteria
5. **Q&A Session:** Address any questions or concerns from Customer Success team
6. **Ownership Transfer:** Formal acknowledgment of responsibility transfer

### 3.3 Post-Handoff Activities
#### Sales Responsibilities:
- Update CRM to reflect handoff completion
- Remain available for client questions during transition period (first 2 weeks)
- Participate in initial kickoff call if requested by CCO
- Document any sales-specific commitments made during sales process
- Provide final opportunity closure report

#### Customer Success Responsibilities:
- Acknowledge receipt of handoff package within 24 hours
- Schedule internal kickoff meeting within 3 business days
- Initiate client onboarding process within 5 business days
- Assign dedicated implementation manager or team lead
- Confirm receipt of all required handoff elements
- Report any missing or incomplete elements to Sales within 48 hours

## 4. Handoff Quality Gates

The handoff is considered complete only when:

### 4.1 Documentation Completeness
- All required handoff package elements are present and organized
- Client contact information is verified and complete
- Solution configuration matches contracted deliverables
- Implementation requirements are clearly defined and feasible

### 4.2 Knowledge Transfer
- Sales has transferred all relevant client context and history
- Customer Success demonstrates understanding of client business objectives
- Open items or concerns from sales process are documented and communicated
- Relationship map shows clear understanding of stakeholder roles

### 4.3 Process Compliance
- Handoff meeting has been conducted and documented
- CRM reflects accurate stage progression and handoff timestamp
- Both Sales and Customer Success have confirmed handoff completion
- No blocking issues or unresolved concerns remain

## 5. Handoff Timeline Standards

### 5.1 Ideal Timeline
- Contract Execution → Handoff Notification: Same day
- Handoff Notification → Handoff Meeting: ≤ 2 business days
- Handoff Meeting → Customer Success Kickoff: ≤ 3 business days
- Customer Success Kickoff → Client Onboarding Initiation: ≤ 5 business days

### 5.2 Escalation Triggers
- Handoff package incomplete after 48 hours: Escalate to CSO and CCO
- Handoff meeting not scheduled within 3 business days: Escalate to CSO
- Client onboarding not initiated within 10 business days of contract execution: Escalate to CEO
- Missing critical information causing implementation delay: Joint escalation to CSO and CCO

## 6. Handoff Metrics and Monitoring

### 6.1 Key Performance Indicators
- **Handoff Timeliness:** Percentage of handoffs completed within ideal timeline
- **Handoff Completeness:** Percentage of handoff packages with all required elements
- **Customer Success Satisfaction:** Post-handoff survey feedback from CCO team
- **Implementation Start Variance:** Difference between planned and actual implementation start
- **Early Stage Issues:** Number of issues traced back to incomplete handoff

### 6.2 Reporting Requirements
- Weekly handoff compliance report shared with CSO and CCO
- Monthly handoff quality review in sales and customer success leadership meeting
- Quarterly handoff process improvement review
- Real-time dashboard showing handoff pipeline status
