- Service recovery and retention support
- Customer feedback capture
- Internal routing of customer signal

## Customer Value Chain

1. **Post-sale handoff** — Receive closed-won account from Sales with complete handoff package
2. **Onboarding** — Set up account, introduce process, align expectations
3. **Delivery setup** — Configure delivery path, confirm timelines, assign owner
4. **Customer communication** — Maintain structured, traceable communication cadence
5. **Issue resolution** — Receive, triage, resolve, or escalate customer issues
6. **Outcome support** — Ensure customer achieves intended value from the product
7. **Renewal support** — Prepare account for renewal, surface retention signals
8. **Feedback routing** — Capture and route customer signal to Product and Technology
9. **Service improvement** — Close the loop, update standards, reduce repeat issues

## Key Workflows

1. **Closed-Won → Handoff → Onboarding → Delivery**
2. **Customer Issue → Triage → Resolve/Escalate → Close → Log**
3. **Feedback Capture → Triage → Route to Product/Technology → Close Loop**
4. **Renewal Approach → Account Review → Renewal Support → Handoff**
