## Departmental Mandate

The CCO is responsible for converting signed business into successful onboarding, reliable delivery, support quality, retention, and customer trust.

The CCO owns what happens after the customer says yes.

## What the CCO Owns

- Onboarding and account setup
- Delivery operations and customer communication
- Issue handling and support quality
- Service recovery and retention support
- Customer feedback capture
- Internal routing of customer signal
- Customer workflow discipline

## What the CCO Does NOT Own

- Top-of-funnel demand creation
- Opportunity closing
- Roadmap prioritisation
- Engineering implementation
- Financial policy

## Customer Value Chain

1. Post-sale handoff (from Sales)
2. Onboarding
3. Delivery setup
4. Customer communication
5. Issue resolution
6. Outcome support
7. Renewal support
8. Feedback routing
9. Service improvement

## Operating Standards

All Customer work follows:
- Company Operating Rules (00_company)

## Cross-Functional Handoff Rules

- **From Sales (CSO):** Closed-won handoff package must include client details, commercial terms, expectations, and contacts
- **To Product (CPO):** Customer feedback, feature requests, and product improvement signals
- **To Technology (CTO):** Delivery issues, quality problems, and technical support escalations
- **To Marketing (CMO):** Customer stories, testimonials, and churn signals
- **To Finance (CFO):** Renewal status, payment issues, and account health

## Reporting Cadence

- Weekly: Customer health and delivery status to CEO
- Monthly: Retention metrics and feedback summary
- Quarterly: Service quality review and improvement plan

## Audit Schedule

Weekly compliance audit: Every Monday
Report to: CEO

## Key Workflows

1. **Sale Closed → Handoff → Onboarding → Active Client**
2. **Client Request → Issue Handling → Resolution → Feedback**
3. **Delivery Cycle → Communication → Reporting → Outcome**
4. **At-Risk Account → Recovery → Retention → Renewal**
